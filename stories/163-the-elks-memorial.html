THE ELKS MEMORIAL

This looks like Rome. It's on Diversey Parkway.

◉ Elks National Memorial, Lincoln Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Dedicated July 14, 1926, overlooking Lincoln Park

  2. ★

    Built to honor about 70,000 Elks who served in World War I

  3. ★

    24 marble columns ring the rotunda below the dome

  4. ★

    Rotunda murals by painter Eugene Savage

  5. ★

    Gilded bronze statues by sculptor James Earle Fraser

Chicago's most beautiful interior, or does another place win?

PRIMARY SOURCES & CREDITS

Sources: Elks.org; City of Chicago landmark report; Elks Magazine; Smithsonian Archives of American Art.

Images: Images: Another Believer, Marilyn Engwall (CC BY-SA 4.0); Chicago Architectural Photographing Co. c.1926, postcard 1941 (PD).

Full source list
  • Dedicated July 14, 1926; site overlooking Lincoln Park — elks.org/memorial/historicaltour.cfm + City of Chicago landmark report (reports.chicagolandmarkreports.org/elks-national-memorial-headquarters-building-l01.pdf).
  • Honors ~70,000 Elks who served in WWI — elks.org ("approximately seventy thousand members entered the war") + City report ("nearly 70,000 Elks members who served").
  • 24 marble columns ring the rotunda below the dome — City report (24 Corinthian columns, second tier, to base of dome) + Elks Magazine Nov 2021 p.38 (ring of 24 marble columns below dome inscription).
  • Rotunda murals by Eugene Savage — City report + Smithsonian Archives of American Art survey (Savage mural series); Elks Magazine also.
  • Gilded bronze rotunda statues by James Earle Fraser — Elks Magazine Nov 2021 (Charity, Brotherly Love, Fidelity, Justice) + Smithsonian AAA (bronze sculptures by James Earle Fraser) + City report (artist list).
