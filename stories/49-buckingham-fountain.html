BUCKINGHAM FOUNTAIN

Opened in 1927, Grant Park's great fountain was built to stand for Lake Michigan.

◉ Grant Park (map pin approximate)

Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Buckingham Fountain opened to the public on May 26, 1927, and was formally dedicated on August 26, 1927, in front of a crowd reported at about 50,000 people.

  2. ★

    Kate Sturges Buckingham gave the fountain to Chicago in memory of her brother, Clarence Buckingham, an art collector and Art Institute trustee who died in 1913.

  3. ★

    Architect Edward H. Bennett designed it, with French sculptor Marcel Loyau and engineer Jacques Lambert. It was inspired by the Latona Fountain at Versailles, but is about twice its size.

  4. ★

    The fountain symbolizes Lake Michigan. Its four pairs of bronze sea horses stand for the four states that border the lake: Illinois, Wisconsin, Michigan and Indiana.

  5. ★

    Built of pink Georgia marble, it is one of the largest fountains in the world. Its central jet can shoot water up to about 150 feet into the air.
