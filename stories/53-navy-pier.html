NAVY PIER

It opened in 1916 as Municipal Pier — a dock for ships that became Chicago's lakefront landmark.

◉ Streeterville (map pin approximate)

Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The pier opened to the public on July 15, 1916, as Municipal Pier. Architect Charles Sumner Frost designed it, based on the 1909 Plan of Chicago.

  2. ★

    It was built for both shipping and fun: freight and passenger docks, plus halls and an auditorium. It rests on more than 20,000 wood pilings and stretches about 3,300 feet into Lake Michigan.

  3. ★

    In 1927 Municipal Pier was renamed Navy Pier, honoring the Navy personnel who were housed there during World War I. In World War II the Navy used it again as a training center.

  4. ★

    From 1946 to 1965 the pier was the Chicago campus of the University of Illinois. More than 100,000 students took classes there.

  5. ★

    The headhouse and east-end auditorium became a Chicago Landmark on November 14, 1977. After a major redevelopment, Navy Pier reopened to the public in July 1995.
