ILLINOIS HAS CANYONS?

Illinois has 18 CANYONS?

◉ Starved Rock State Park, Utica (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Starved Rock State Park, on the Illinois River, has 18 canyons cut into St. Peter Sandstone.

  2. ★

    Geologists say glacial meltwater floods helped carve them about 15,000 to 19,000 years ago.

  3. ★

    Illinois bought the land in 1911, making it the state’s second state park.

  4. ★

    Its log-and-stone lodge was built in the 1930s by the Civilian Conservation Corps.

  5. ★

    In 2024 it drew about 2.4 million visitors, the most of any Illinois state park.
