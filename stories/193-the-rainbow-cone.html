THE RAINBOW CONE

Joseph and Katherine Sapp opened a shop in Beverly in 1926

◉ 9233 S. Western Ave. (Original Rainbow Cone, Beverly) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 6 FACTS

  1. ★

    Joseph and Katherine Sapp opened a shop in Beverly in 1926

  2. ★

    It began at 92nd Street and Western Avenue

  3. ★

    The shop is still open nearby, at 9233 South Western Avenue

  4. ★

    The cone is five flavors, sliced in a stack, not scooped

  5. ★

    Bottom to top: chocolate, strawberry, Palmer House, pistachio, and orange sherbet

  6. ★

    Palmer House is vanilla with walnuts and cherries
