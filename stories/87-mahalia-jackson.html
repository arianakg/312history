MAHALIA JACKSON

A teenager from New Orleans came to Chicago, sang in its churches, and became a gospel star heard around the world.

◉ Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1927, at 16, Mahalia Jackson moved from New Orleans to Chicago and sang in Baptist churches there.

  2. ★

    Her 1947 Apollo record "Move On Up a Little Higher" made her a national star. Reported sales range from two million to eight million.

  3. ★

    In 1961 she sang at events for President John F. Kennedy's inauguration.

  4. ★

    In 1963 she sang at the March on Washington, the rally where Martin Luther King Jr. gave his "I Have a Dream" speech.

  5. ★

    She died on January 27, 1972, at age 60.
