THE 1911 CHICAGO AVIATION MEET

In August 1911, airplanes filled the sky over Chicago's lakefront.

◉ Grant Park lakefront (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The International Aviation Meet was held August 12–20, 1911, in Grant Park along Lake Michigan.

  2. ★

    Pilots at the meet included American Lincoln Beachey and Englishman Thomas Sopwith.

  3. ★

    Reports at the time estimated that about 300,000 people watched.

  4. ★

    Beachey set a world altitude record at the meet in his Curtiss biplane.

  5. ★

    Two pilots, St. Croix Johnstone and William Badger, died in crashes during the meet.
