FROM STEEL TO WETLANDS

Endangered birds live HERE?

◉ Lake Calumet wetlands, Southeast Side (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1912, a student found a tiny flower, Thismia americana, near Lake Calumet. No one has seen it alive since 1916.

  2. ★

    For years, steel slag was dumped at Big Marsh on the Southeast Side.

  3. ★

    In 2016, a 40-acre bike park opened on Big Marsh’s old industrial land.

  4. ★

    Black-crowned night herons, endangered in Illinois, have nested in the marshes around Lake Calumet.

  5. ★

    Rare yellow-headed blackbirds nest at Hegewisch Marsh and have returned to Big Marsh.
