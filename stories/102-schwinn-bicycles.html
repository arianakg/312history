SCHWINN BICYCLES

From 1895 until the early 1980s, Schwinn bicycles were built in Chicago.

◉ Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In the 1930s, Schwinn brought fat, double-tube balloon tires to its bikes.

  2. ★

    In 1941, a French racer rode a Schwinn Paramount past 100 mph in a motor-paced speed record.

  3. ★

    In 1949, Schwinn introduced the Black Phantom, a chrome-trimmed cruiser.

  4. ★

    By 1950, about one in four bicycles sold in the U.S. was a Schwinn.

  5. ★

    In 1963, Schwinn launched the Sting-Ray, with high-rise handlebars and a banana seat.
