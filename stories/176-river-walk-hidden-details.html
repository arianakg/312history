RIVER WALK: HIDDEN DETAILS

A free Loop walk, about 60–90 min with stops (our estimate, ~1.5 mi).

◉ Chicago Riverwalk, Michigan Ave & Wacker Dr (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Bronze sidewalk markers at Michigan & Wacker trace Fort Dearborn (1803)

  2. ★

    The bridge opened May 14, 1920; its history reliefs came in 1928

  3. ★

    About 150 fragments from sites worldwide sit in Tribune Tower's walls

  4. ★

    Marina City's towers start with spiral parking ramps

  5. ★

    Riverwalk: free, 6 am–11 pm

Start: Lake Red Line. End: Clark/Lake. CTA fare extra. Route uses stairs. Check CTA alerts and Riverwalk access first.
