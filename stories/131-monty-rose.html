MONTY & ROSE

Two endangered shorebirds, from only about 70 Great Lakes pairs, picked a busy Chicago beach.

◉ Montrose Beach (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In July 2019, piping plover chicks hatched in Chicago for the first time since 1955.

  2. ★

    Their parents were nicknamed Monty and Rose, after Montrose Beach.

  3. ★

    High lake water flooded the first nest. The eggs were rescued but did not develop.

  4. ★

    In a second nest, three chicks hatched. Two grew up and flew.

  5. ★

    That August, a beach music festival was canceled, partly because of the plovers.
