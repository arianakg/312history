FLORENCE KELLEY

In 1890s Chicago, a Hull House resident became the state's chief factory inspector and took on the sweatshops.

◉ Hull House (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1891 Florence Kelley came to Chicago with her three children and began working at Hull House.

  2. ★

    In 1893 Illinois Governor John P. Altgeld appointed her chief factory inspector, enforcing the state's new Factory Inspection Law.

  3. ★

    While serving as inspector, she earned a law degree from Northwestern University.

  4. ★

    In 1899 she moved to New York to lead the National Consumers League. She stayed with it for more than 30 years.

  5. ★

    She died on February 17, 1932.
