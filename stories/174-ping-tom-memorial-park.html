PING TOM MEMORIAL PARK

A Chinatown park grew on an old rail yard by the Chicago River.

◉ Ping Tom Memorial Park, Chinatown (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    It was built on a former railroad yard along the South Branch of the Chicago River

  2. ★

    Chinatown lost its nearby parks in the 1960s to make way for the Dan Ryan Expressway

  3. ★

    The park opened in 1999 and is named for Chinatown civic leader Ping Tom

  4. ★

    Its boathouse opened in 2013, the first of the city's new Chicago River boathouses

  5. ★

    Next door, a 1914–15 rail lift bridge had the heaviest US lift span when finished
