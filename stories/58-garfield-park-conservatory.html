GARFIELD PARK CONSERVATORY

Opened in 1908 on Chicago's West Side, it was built as "landscape art under glass."

◉ East Garfield Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Built from 1906 and opened in 1908, it was designed by landscape architect Jens Jensen with the greenhouse firm Hitchings & Company.

  2. ★

    It replaced three small Victorian-era greenhouses in Garfield, Douglas and Humboldt parks with one central conservatory.

  3. ★

    Jensen shaped the front Palm House after the haystacks of the Midwestern prairie, instead of a palace or villa.

  4. ★

    Instead of rows of potted plants, he planted directly in the ground, with lagoons, paths and layered stonework.

  5. ★

    In the 1940s and 1950s much of it was reglazed with fiberglass; a restoration around 2000 brought back glass roofs.
