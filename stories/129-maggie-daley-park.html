MAGGIE DALEY PARK

Downtown Chicago has a park where you skate a loop instead of a rink.

◉ Maggie Daley Park, Grant Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The skating ribbon opened on December 13, 2014. The whole park opened in 2015.

  2. ★

    The park sits on top of an underground parking garage.

  3. ★

    Its hills are built from geofoam, about 100 times lighter than soil.

  4. ★

    The skating ribbon is nearly a quarter of a mile long.

  5. ★

    Maggie Daley Park covers 20 acres in the north end of Grant Park.
