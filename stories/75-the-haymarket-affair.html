THE HAYMARKET AFFAIR

In May 1886, a Chicago labor meeting near Haymarket Square ended with a bomb, and eight men went on trial.

◉ Haymarket Square, Randolph & Des Plaines (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    On May 3, 1886, during the fight for an eight-hour workday, Chicago police fired on strikers at the McCormick Reaper Works, killing and wounding several men. A handbill called a protest meeting for the next night.

  2. ★

    On May 4, the meeting gathered near Haymarket Square on Randolph Street. As police moved in to break it up, someone threw a dynamite bomb, and gunfire followed. At least seven officers died, and an unknown number of civilians were killed or hurt.

  3. ★

    The bomb thrower was never identified. Eight anarchists were tried for murder anyway, and in August 1886 all eight were convicted.

  4. ★

    On November 11, 1887, four of them were hanged. A fifth died in his cell the day before.

  5. ★

    On June 26, 1893, Governor John Peter Altgeld pardoned the three still in prison, writing that the trial had not been fair. It hurt his career.
