LINCOLN PARK ZOO

It began in 1868 with a gift of swans — and grew into one of America's oldest zoos, still free.

◉ Lincoln Park (map pin approximate)

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Lincoln Park Zoo began in 1868, when the commissioners of New York's Central Park sent Chicago a gift: two pairs of swans.

  2. ★

    The swans were a hit, and more animals followed. In 1874 the zoo bought its first animal — a bear cub from the Philadelphia Zoo, for $10.

  3. ★

    In 1878 the Lincoln Park commissioners declared that the zoo must always remain free. It is still free today, one of the oldest zoos in the country.

  4. ★

    In 1884 a bison calf was born at the zoo, reported as one of the first bison born in captivity. The Bird House opened in 1904 and is the zoo's oldest animal house still in use.

  5. ★

    The Lion House opened in 1912. Marlin Perkins, zoo director from 1944 to 1962, brought the animals to television on the show Zoo Parade.
