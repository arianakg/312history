THE BURNHAM PLAN, 1909

In 1909, one bold plan re-drew Chicago — and much of it came true.

◉ Whole city (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In July 1909, Daniel Burnham and Edward Bennett published the Plan of Chicago for the Commercial Club.

  2. ★

    Artist Jules Guérin painted dreamy watercolor views of the future city.

  3. ★

    Many ideas came true: a long chain of lakefront parks, Wacker Drive, the Michigan Avenue bridge and Northerly Island.

  4. ★

    Not all of it came true: the giant domed civic center was never built.

  5. ★

    "Make no little plans" — Burnham said these famous words in London in 1910. They are not in the 1909 Plan.
