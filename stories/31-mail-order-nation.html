Firsts · Post #31
1872

MAIL ORDER NATION

Before online shopping, a Chicago catalog let a farm family buy almost anything — even a house.

◉ Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In August 1872, Aaron Montgomery Ward started his Chicago mail-order business. His first catalog was a single sheet listing 163 items, with instructions for ordering by mail.

  2. ★

    Richard W. Sears moved his mail-order watch business to Chicago in 1887 and hired watchmaker Alvah C. Roebuck. Sears, Roebuck and Co. followed in 1893, and its first large general catalog came in 1896.

  3. ★

    Chicago was a railroad crossroads. Ward company records call the city a terminus for many railroads and ideally placed for distribution, so orders could go out by rail to customers far away.

  4. ★

    Rural Free Delivery began as an experiment in 1896 and became permanent in 1902, bringing mail and catalogs to farm doors. Parcel Post began on January 1, 1913, so the Post Office could deliver packages too.

  5. ★

    The big catalogs sold clothes, tools, furniture and much more. From 1908 to 1940, Sears Modern Homes even sold house kits by catalog: the first specialty house catalog appeared in 1908, and the last in 1940.
