THE FIRST FERRIS WHEEL, 1893

Chicago needed something to beat Paris's Eiffel Tower. An engineer from Pittsburgh had an idea.

◉ Midway Plaisance (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    George W. G. Ferris's wheel took its first riders on June 21, 1893, at the World's Columbian Exposition: 250 ft across and about 264 ft tall.

  2. ★

    36 cars, each with 40 seats and room for up to 60 people.

  3. ★

    50 cents bought two turns. The wheel paid for itself by September 1, 1893.

  4. ★

    It was rebuilt on the North Side in 1895, shipped to St. Louis for the 1904 fair, and dynamited in 1906.

Myth or fact? "The wheel made George Ferris a rich man." Myth. Lawsuits over profits and a hard economy ruined him. He died in 1896, aged 37, nearly broke.
