THE 1916 SUFFRAGE MARCH

On June 7, 1916, about 5,000 women marched through pouring rain to the Republican convention in Chicago.

◉ Michigan Ave to the Coliseum (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    On June 7, 1916, suffragists marched down Michigan Avenue to the Coliseum, where the Republican National Convention was meeting.

  2. ★

    The march was organized by the National American Woman Suffrage Association, led by Carrie Chapman Catt, to win a suffrage plank in the party platform.

  3. ★

    Heavy rain fell all day, and about 5,000 women marched anyway.

  4. ★

    The marchers entered the hall while a convention committee was holding a hearing on the platform.

  5. ★

    The 1916 Republican platform included a plank favoring votes for women.
