THE ARAGON BALLROOM

In July 1926, the Aragon Ballroom opened on Lawrence Avenue in Uptown as a dance hall.

◉ Aragon Ballroom, Uptown (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Aragon Ballroom opened in July 1926 near Lawrence and Broadway, built for about $2 million.

  2. ★

    Its interior was designed to look like the courtyard of a Spanish palace, with arches and balconies.

  3. ★

    Overhead, the ceiling had twinkling stars and floating clouds.

  4. ★

    The dance floor sat on springs, cushioning dancers’ feet.

  5. ★

    After the big-band era faded, the Aragon was hosting rock concerts by the 1970s.
