THE TV REMOTE

Did you know the TV remote was invented in Chicago?

◉ 6001 W. Dickens Ave., Chicago (former Zenith Radio Corporation plant) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Did you know the TV remote was invented in Chicago?

  2. ★

    In 1950, Zenith Radio Corporation made a wired remote called Lazy Bones.

  3. ★

    In 1955, Eugene Polley's Flash-Matic used a light beam. It was the first wireless TV remote.

  4. ★

    In 1956, Robert Adler's Space Command used ultrasound.

  5. ★

    Zenith built it in Chicago, at its plant on West Dickens Avenue.
