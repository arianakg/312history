BENNY GOODMAN

A boy from Chicago's West Side learned clarinet at a synagogue and a settlement house, then became the 'King of Swing.'

◉ Hull House (map pin approximate)

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Benny Goodman was born in Chicago on May 30, 1909, to immigrant parents.

  2. ★

    In 1919 he began clarinet lessons at a Chicago synagogue, and he played in the boys' band at Jane Addams's Hull House.

  3. ★

    On August 21, 1935, his band played the Palomar Ballroom in Los Angeles. The night is often described as the start of the swing era.

  4. ★

    On January 16, 1938, his orchestra played a jazz concert at New York's Carnegie Hall.

  5. ★

    He kept performing until shortly before his death on June 13, 1986.
