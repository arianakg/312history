THE BUD BILLIKEN PARADE

Since 1929, the Bud Billiken Parade has sent South Side kids back to school each August.

◉ Bronzeville to Washington Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Chicago Defender newspaper held the first Bud Billiken Parade on August 11, 1929.

  2. ★

    Its name comes from the Billiken, a 1908 good-luck charm figure that the Defender presented as a guardian of children.

  3. ★

    It is a back-to-school tradition, held on the second Saturday in August.

  4. ★

    The parade travels down Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Drive through Bronzeville and ends with a picnic in Washington Park.

  5. ★

    From the start, the aim was to give underprivileged kids a chance to be in the limelight for a day.
