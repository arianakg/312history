★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    911: emergencies. 311: city services and police non-emergency reports, 24/7.

  2. ★

    988: Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Call or text, free and confidential, 24/7.

  3. ★

    1-800-222-1222: Illinois Poison Center.

  4. ★

    ComEd power outage or downed line: 1-800-334-7661. Smell gas? Leave the area, then call Peoples Gas: 866-556-6002.

  5. ★

    CTA customer service: 1-888-968-7282 (1-888-YOUR-CTA). Food, rent and other local help: 211 (or text your ZIP to 898211).
