THE WRAPPED MUSEUM

Chicago WRAPPED a whole museum?

◉ Museum of Contemporary Art, Streeterville (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Museum of Contemporary Art opened in 1967 in a former bakery on East Ontario Street.

  2. ★

    In 1969, Christo wrapped the museum in canvas, the first building he wrapped in the U.S.

  3. ★

    In 1996, it moved to the site of the old National Guard Armory on East Chicago Avenue.

  4. ★

    Berlin architect Josef Paul Kleihues designed the new building, clad in aluminum and limestone.

  5. ★

    It opened in June 1996 with a free, nonstop 24-hour summer solstice preview.
