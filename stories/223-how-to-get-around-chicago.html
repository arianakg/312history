HOW TO GET AROUND CHICAGO

How do you get around Chicago?

◉ The Loop, Chicago (Clark/Lake station) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 6 FACTS

  1. ★

    The L is Chicago's train. It has 8 color lines.

  2. ★

    Blue goes to O'Hare. Orange goes to Midway. Red and Blue run 24 hours.

  3. ★

    CTA buses cover the city. The L fare is $2.50. The bus fare is $2.25 with Ventra or a tap.

  4. ★

    Pay once, then ride free: up to 2 more rides within 2 hours are free with Ventra or a tap. Not with cash on the bus. A 1-Day CTA/Pace pass is $5. From O'Hare, the L is $5.

  5. ★

    Metra trains go to the suburbs. Fares depend on how far you ride. Buy a ticket in the Ventra app.

  6. ★

    Pay with a Ventra card, the Ventra app, or a tap of a contactless card or phone. The Loop L opened in 1897.

Fares checked Nov. 18, 2026, on transitchicago.com. Confirm in the Ventra app before you ride, because fares can change.
