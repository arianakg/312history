THE EASTLAND, 1915

Chicago's deadliest day happened in the river, steps from the shore.

◉ Chicago River at Clark St (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    On the morning of July 24, 1915, Western Electric workers and their families boarded the SS Eastland for a company picnic trip to Michigan City, Indiana.

  2. ★

    The ship had reached its capacity of about 2,500 passengers.

  3. ★

    At 7:28 a.m., still tied up in the Chicago River between Clark and LaSalle, it rolled onto its side in about 20 ft of water.

  4. ★

    844 people died, including 22 entire families. Many were young immigrant workers.

Myth or fact? "The Eastland sank far out in Lake Michigan." Myth. It never left the dock. It overturned in the river, in the heart of the city, still tied to the wharf.
