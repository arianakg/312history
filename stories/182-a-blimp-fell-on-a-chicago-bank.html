A BLIMP FELL ON A CHICAGO BANK

In 1919 an airship caught fire over the Loop. It crashed through a bank's glass roof. People inside were still at work.

◉ Illinois Trust and Savings Bank, LaSalle St. & Jackson Blvd. (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Wingfoot Air Express caught fire over the Loop on July 21, 1919

  2. ★

    It crashed through the glass roof of the Illinois Trust and Savings Bank

  3. ★

    People inside the bank were still at work

  4. ★

    Thirteen people died

  5. ★

    The city made new flying rules

Photos: New-York Tribune, 1919 (PD); Detroit Photographic Co., ca. 1900 (PD). Music: original piano. Sources: Chicago Public Library; University of Illinois Press; Chicago Sun-Times (from the Chicago Daily News).
