THE OLD FEDERAL BUILDING

For 60 years, a domed palace in the Loop handled Chicago's mail and major federal trials.

◉ Loop, Dearborn St (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Designed by Henry Ives Cobb and completed in 1905, the Federal Building filled the block of Adams, Clark, Dearborn and Jackson, housing the federal courts and the main post office.

  2. ★

    Shaped like a Greek cross on a high base, it was crowned by a great dome.

  3. ★

    In 1918, in a federal courtroom in Chicago, Judge Kenesaw Mountain Landis tried more than 100 leaders of the IWW labor union and sent them to prison.

  4. ★

    On September 4, 1918, a bomb exploded at the building's Adams Street entrance, killing four people. No one was ever convicted.

  5. ★

    The building was demolished in 1965 to make way for the Chicago Federal Center designed by Mies van der Rohe.
