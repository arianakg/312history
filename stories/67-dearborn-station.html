DEARBORN STATION

Chicago's oldest surviving train station still stands at the south end of Dearborn Street, clock tower and all.

◉ Printers Row (map pin approximate)

Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Dearborn Station opened in May 1885, designed by New York architect Cyrus L. W. Eidlitz in pink granite and red brick.

  2. ★

    At its peak it handled about 17,000 passengers a day, with the Santa Fe as its main railroad.

  3. ★

    On December 21, 1922, a fire destroyed its steep roofs and upper floor. No one was hurt.

  4. ★

    The Santa Fe rebuilt it with flat roofs, and trains kept running until Amtrak moved service to Union Station in 1971.

  5. ★

    Its train shed came down in 1976, but the station became shops and offices in the 1980s and is a Chicago Landmark.
