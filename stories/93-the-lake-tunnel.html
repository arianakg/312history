THE LAKE TUNNEL

In the 1860s, Chicago dug a tunnel under Lake Michigan for its drinking water.

◉ Lake Michigan (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In March 1864, work began on a tunnel to bring cleaner water to Chicago from Lake Michigan.

  2. ★

    The tunnel ran about two miles out from shore, under the lake bed.

  3. ★

    In 1865 a large timber "crib," built on land, was towed out onto the lake and sunk to form the far shaft.

  4. ★

    Crews dug toward each other from the shore and from the crib, and the two tunnels met in late 1866.

  5. ★

    In March 1867, lake water began flowing through the tunnel to the city.
