THE MILLION-DOLLAR SERMON

A Chicago meatpacker heard a sermon about a million dollars, and paid for a whole school.

◉ Armour Institute (IIT) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Philip D. Armour was born on a New York farm in 1832. He built Armour & Co. into one of Chicago's biggest meatpacking firms.

  2. ★

    In 1886 he opened the Armour Mission, a community center with classes and activities for children.

  3. ★

    In 1890 preacher Frank W. Gunsaulus gave what became known as the "Million Dollar Sermon": with $1 million, he said, he could build a new kind of school. Armour offered the money.

  4. ★

    Armour Institute opened in 1893, teaching engineering, architecture and library science, with Gunsaulus as its first president.

  5. ★

    Armour died in January 1901. In 1940 his school merged with Lewis Institute to form today's Illinois Institute of Technology.
