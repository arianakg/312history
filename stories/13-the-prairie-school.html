THE PRAIRIE SCHOOL

Low roofs, long lines: a new American house was born around Chicago.

◉ Oak Park / Hyde Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    Frank Lloyd Wright built his Oak Park home in 1889 and added a studio in 1898. Over 150 projects were designed there.

  2. ★

    Prairie houses stress long, low lines, deep overhanging roofs and bands of windows that echo the flat Midwest.

  3. ★

    The Robie House (designed 1908, finished 1910) is the high point of the style.

  4. ★

    In 2019 Robie House and Oak Park's Unity Temple joined UNESCO's World Heritage List with six other Wright works.

Myth or fact? "Wright created the Prairie School alone." Partly. Wright led it, but his studio team, including Marion Mahony (the first practicing woman architect in Illinois) and Walter Burley Griffin, shaped it too.
