PUBLIC ENEMY NUMBER 1

Did you know Public Enemy Number 1 died outside a Chicago movie theater?

◉ The Biograph Theater, 2433 N. Lincoln Avenue, Chicago (Lincoln Park)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 6 FACTS

  1. ★

    Did you know Public Enemy Number 1 died outside a Chicago movie theater?

  2. ★

    On July 22, 1934, John Dillinger went to a movie in Chicago

  3. ★

    The theater was the Biograph, on Lincoln Avenue

  4. ★

    Federal agents were waiting outside

  5. ★

    He was shot as he left, and he died that night

  6. ★

    The Biograph Theater is still on Lincoln Avenue

PRIMARY SOURCES & CREDITS

Full source list
  • FBI, "John Dillinger." Sunday night, July 22, 1934, at the Biograph Theater in Chicago; Dillinger went in to see Manhattan Melodrama; agents waited outside on J. Edgar Hoover's instruction; he was shot as he left and was pronounced dead at 10:50 p.m. at Alexian Brothers Hospital. https://www.fbi.gov/history/famous-cases/john-dillinger
  • FBI, "FBI 100: Dillinger's Last Steps," July 22, 2008. Public Enemy Number 1; a tip led Melvin Purvis of the Chicago office to the Biograph on a hot Sunday night; Dillinger was killed moments after leaving the theater. https://www.fbi.gov/news/stories/2008/july/dillinger_072208
  • City of Chicago, Department of Planning and Development, Landmark Designation Report, Biograph Theater, 2433–43 N. Lincoln Avenue. Built 1914, architect Samuel N. Crowen; Dillinger was shot outside the theater on July 22, 1934, after watching Manhattan Melodrama; designated a Chicago Landmark on March 28, 2001. https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/zlup/Historic_Preservation/Publications/Biograph_Theater.pdf
  • U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder, 2433 N. Lincoln Ave., Chicago, IL 60614, November 19, 2026: 41.92622, -87.64974.
