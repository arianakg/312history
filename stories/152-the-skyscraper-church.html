THE SKYSCRAPER CHURCH

The world’s tallest CHURCH is in the Loop?

◉ Chicago Temple, 77 W. Washington St. (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Chicago Temple, finished in 1924, is a 568-foot skyscraper with a church inside.

  2. ★

    It is often called the tallest church building in the world.

  3. ★

    Its congregation began meeting in 1831, before Chicago became a city in 1837.

  4. ★

    The Sky Chapel, about 400 feet up under the spire, opened in 1952, a gift of the Walgreen family.

  5. ★

    Holabird & Roche designed it with a sanctuary at street level and offices on the floors above.
