1903

THE IROQUOIS THEATER FIRE

Chicago's newest theater was sold as "absolutely fireproof." Five weeks later it became the deadliest theater fire in U.S. history.

◉ Randolph St (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Iroquois Theater opened on Randolph Street on November 23, 1903, and its owners advertised it as "absolutely fireproof."

  2. ★

    On December 30, 1903, during a packed holiday matinee of Mr. Bluebeard, an arc light set a stage curtain on fire.

  3. ★

    The asbestos fire curtain jammed, and many exits were locked, hidden behind drapes, or opened only inward.

  4. ★

    About 602 people died, mostly women and children, the deadliest single-building fire in U.S. history.

  5. ★

    The disaster led to laws across the country requiring public exit doors to open outward.
