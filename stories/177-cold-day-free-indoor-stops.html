COLD DAY? FREE INDOOR STOPS

Four free places to warm up: separate options, different days, not one route.

◉ Loop indoor stops (Cultural Center, library, Fed, Newberry) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    Chicago Cultural Center: the 1897 former central library, with a Tiffany dome. Daily 10 am–5 pm

  2. ★

    Winter Garden: glass-roofed 9th-floor hall at Harold Washington Library; may close for events

  3. ★

    Money Museum: modern exhibit at the Chicago Fed. Mon–Fri 10 am–5 pm; photo ID 18+; closed Dec 7–mid-Jan

  4. ★

    Newberry Library: free galleries in its 1893 home. Tue–Thu 10 am–7 pm, Fri–Sat 10 am–5 pm

Hours checked Nov 8, 2026; holidays vary, confirm before you go.
