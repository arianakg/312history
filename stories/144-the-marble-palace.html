THE MARBLE PALACE

17 kinds of MARBLE in one house?

◉ Driehaus Museum, River North (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Banker Samuel Nickerson’s mansion was finished in 1883, after the 1871 Great Fire burned his earlier home.

  2. ★

    It was built as one of the first fireproof houses in Chicago.

  3. ★

    Nicknamed the “Marble Palace,” it is lined with 17 types of marble.

  4. ★

    In 1919, Chicago citizens bought it and gave it to the American College of Surgeons.

  5. ★

    Richard Driehaus bought it in 2003 and, after five years of restoration, opened it as a museum in 2008.
