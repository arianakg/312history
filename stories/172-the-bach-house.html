THE BACH HOUSE

Frank Lloyd Wright built a brickmaker a Prairie cube on Sheridan Road.

◉ Bach House, Sheridan Road, Rogers Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Frank Lloyd Wright designed it in 1915 for Emil Bach of the Bach Brick Company

  2. ★

    Late Prairie style: a compact cubic house with a flat slab roof

  3. ★

    One of Wright's last designs for a small urban house

  4. ★

    Chicago Landmark on September 28, 1977

  5. ★

    National Register of Historic Places on January 23, 1979
