THE ORIENTAL INSTITUTE

In Hyde Park, a museum was built around a 40-ton winged bull from ancient Assyria.

◉ Oriental Institute, Hyde Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1919 John D. Rockefeller Jr. funded Egyptologist James Henry Breasted's plan, and the University of Chicago created the Oriental Institute.

  2. ★

    In 1929 the institute's archaeologists uncovered a human-headed winged bull, or lamassu, at Khorsabad in northern Iraq.

  3. ★

    In 1930 its pieces were brought into the new building through a gallery wall while it was still under construction. It stands about 16 feet tall and weighs about 40 tons.

  4. ★

    The institute's permanent building at 1155 E. 58th St. opened in 1931.

  5. ★

    In April 2023 it was renamed the Institute for the Study of Ancient Cultures, West Asia & North Africa (ISAC).
