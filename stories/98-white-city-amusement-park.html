WHITE CITY AMUSEMENT PARK

In 1905, a South Side cornfield became the White City amusement park.

◉ White City, 63rd & South Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    White City opened on May 27, 1905, at 63rd Street and South Park Avenue, today's Martin Luther King Drive.

  2. ★

    Its Electric Tower was covered in lights and could be seen from about 15 miles away.

  3. ★

    Rides included the Shoot the Chutes water ride and several roller coasters.

  4. ★

    A popular fire show set model buildings ablaze while fire engines raced to the rescue.

  5. ★

    Hit by fires and the Depression, the park went into receivership over unpaid taxes in 1933.
