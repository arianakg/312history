BRONZEVILLE & THE GREAT MIGRATION

Half a million Black Southerners came to Chicago, and built a city within a city.

◉ Bronzeville (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    From 1916 to 1970, just over 500,000 African Americans moved to Chicago from the South.

  2. ★

    The Chicago Defender, founded by Robert S. Abbott in 1905, urged readers in the South to come north.

  3. ★

    Chicago's Black population grew from about 44,000 in 1910 to about 234,000 in 1930.

  4. ★

    Shut out by segregation, residents built their own banks, churches, newspapers and theaters: the "Black Metropolis."

  5. ★

    The community gave Bronzeville its own name. James J. Gentry of the Chicago Bee coined it around 1930.
