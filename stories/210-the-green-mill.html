THE GREEN MILL

This Uptown jazz club still lights Broadway with a green neon sign.

◉ 4802 N. Broadway, Uptown, Chicago (Green Mill Cocktail Lounge) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    This Uptown jazz club still lights Broadway with a green neon sign.

  2. ★

    In 1914, Green Mill Gardens opened on this Uptown corner.

  3. ★

    In 1927, after singer Joe E. Lewis left the club, three men attacked him in his hotel.

  4. ★

    In 1986, Marc Smith started the Uptown Poetry Slam on this stage.

  5. ★

    The club is still open at 4802 North Broadway. Live jazz plays there every night.
