THE MUSEUM CAMPUS

In the 1990s, Chicago moved part of Lake Shore Drive so three museums could share one lakefront park.

◉ Museum Campus (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Museum Campus took its present shape in 1998.

  2. ★

    Northbound Lake Shore Drive was moved west of the Field Museum and Soldier Field.

  3. ★

    That joined the Field Museum, Shedd Aquarium and Adler Planetarium in one lakefront park.

  4. ★

    The campus covers 57 acres.

  5. ★

    Parkland and bike paths now run where the old northbound lanes were.
