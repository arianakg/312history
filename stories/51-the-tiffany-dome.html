THE TIFFANY DOME

Built in 1897 as Chicago's first central library, this "people's palace" hides the world's largest Tiffany glass dome.

◉ Michigan Ave (Cultural Center) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The building was completed in 1897 as Chicago's first central public library. It opened to the public in October 1897, after five years of design and construction.

  2. ★

    The Boston firm Shepley, Rutan and Coolidge designed it, in a classical style with rare marbles, polished brass and glittering mosaics.

  3. ★

    Its crown jewel is the Tiffany dome in Preston Bradley Hall: about 38 feet across, with some 30,000 pieces of Favrile glass, cut in the shape of fish scales. It is the largest Tiffany glass dome in the world.

  4. ★

    The building also honored Civil War veterans: its Grand Army of the Republic rooms, completed in 1897, have a second glass dome of their own.

  5. ★

    Chicago made the building a city landmark on November 15, 1976. It became the Chicago Cultural Center in 1991, and in 2008 the Tiffany dome was restored to let natural light shine through again.
