A CENTURY OF PROGRESS, 1933

In the middle of the Great Depression, Chicago threw a world's fair.

◉ Northerly Island (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    It opened May 27, 1933, on Northerly Island and the lakefront for Chicago's 100th birthday: about 424 acres, much of it landfill.

  2. ★

    The Sky Ride: two 628-ft towers, 1,850 ft apart, with "rocket cars" running between them.

  3. ★

    George Fred Keck's glass House of Tomorrow, complete with an airplane hangar, now stands in the Indiana Dunes.

  4. ★

    The fair was so popular it ran a second season in 1934: about 39 million visits in all.

Myth or fact? "The fair was switched on by starlight that left Arcturus in 1893." Partly. Light from Arcturus really did trigger the opening lights. But the star is about 37 light-years away, so that light left around 1896, not during the 1893 fair.
