1891

PROVIDENT HOSPITAL

One nursing student was turned away, so Chicago built a hospital where she could train.

◉ 29th & Dearborn (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Around 1889, Emma Reynolds was refused by every nursing school in Chicago because she was Black. Her brother asked Dr. Daniel Hale Williams for help.

  2. ★

    Williams and a group of Black ministers, doctors and businessmen opened Provident Hospital on May 4, 1891: 12 beds in a house at 29th and Dearborn, open to patients of all races.

  3. ★

    In 1893, Williams operated on James Cornish, who had been stabbed near the heart, and sewed the sac around his heart. Cornish lived for more than 20 years.

  4. ★

    In 1898, Provident moved into a new 65-bed building at 36th and Dearborn. Frederick Douglass gave to the building fund.

  5. ★

    Provident kept training Black nurses for decades, and in 1933 it moved to a larger building on 51st Street.
