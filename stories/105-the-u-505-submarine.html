THE U-505 SUBMARINE

In 1954, a German submarine captured in World War II became an exhibit at Chicago’s Museum of Science and Industry.

◉ Museum of Science and Industry, Hyde Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    On June 4, 1944, a U.S. Navy task group captured the German submarine U-505 off West Africa.

  2. ★

    The Navy kept the capture secret until after the war.

  3. ★

    In 1954, U-505 was towed to Chicago through the St. Lawrence River and the Great Lakes.

  4. ★

    On September 25, 1954, it was dedicated at the Museum of Science and Industry as a war memorial.

  5. ★

    In 2004, the museum moved it into a climate-controlled hall four stories below ground.
