THE 606

In 2015, an old rail line on the Northwest Side became a park in the sky — and its name hides a Chicago number.

◉ The 606, Bloomingdale Trail (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The 606 opened on June 6, 2015 — 6/06/15.

  2. ★

    It is named for the 606 ZIP code prefix all Chicagoans share.

  3. ★

    Its centerpiece is the elevated, 2.7-mile Bloomingdale Trail.

  4. ★

    The trail runs along an old, unused elevated rail line.

  5. ★

    It links four neighborhoods: Bucktown, Wicker Park, Logan Square and Humboldt Park.
