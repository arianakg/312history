BROOKFIELD ZOO & SU-LIN

In 1937, a baby giant panda named Su-Lin became a sensation at Brookfield Zoo.

◉ Brookfield Zoo, Brookfield (west of Chicago) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Brookfield Zoo opened in 1934 with barless exhibits.

  2. ★

    In 1937, Su-Lin, a baby giant panda brought from China, came to the zoo.

  3. ★

    Su-Lin was introduced as a “she,” but turned out to be a male.

  4. ★

    After Su-Lin died in 1938, his body went to Chicago’s Field Museum.

  5. ★

    In 1966, the zoo got its first Mold-A-Rama souvenir machine.
