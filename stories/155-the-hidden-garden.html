THE HIDDEN GARDEN

Chicago has a SECRET garden next to the zoo?

◉ Alfred Caldwell Lily Pool, Lincoln Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Lincoln Park’s Lily Pool began in 1889 as a heated pond for tropical water lilies.

  2. ★

    In 1936–38, Alfred Caldwell rebuilt it as a Prairie-style garden, a Park District project backed by the WPA.

  3. ★

    He shaped it like a stream through a Midwestern prairie, with layered stone like the region’s limestone.

  4. ★

    After decades of decline, it was restored to Caldwell’s design between 2000 and 2002.

  5. ★

    It is now a Chicago Landmark (2002) and a National Historic Landmark (2006).
