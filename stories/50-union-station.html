UNION STATION

Opened in 1925, Chicago's Union Station gave the railroad capital a Great Hall under a glass sky.

◉ West Loop (map pin approximate)

Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Chicago Union Station opened for business on May 15, 1925. A formal grand opening ceremony followed on July 23, 1925.

  2. ★

    Daniel Burnham's firm made the first design. After Burnham died in 1912, the work was finished by his successors, Graham, Anderson, Probst & White.

  3. ★

    The Great Hall is the heart of the station. Its barrel-vaulted skylight is 219 feet long and rises about 115 feet above the floor, over columns of Roman travertine.

  4. ★

    During World War II the skylight was blacked out for security. It was fully restored in 2018, letting natural light back into the hall.

  5. ★

    At its peak the station handled up to 300 trains and 100,000 passengers a day. Chicago named Union Station a city landmark on May 1, 2002.
