JACKSON PARK’S JAPANESE GARDEN

A Japanese garden on Jackson Park’s Wooded Island began with Japan’s pavilion at the 1893 World’s Fair.

◉ Osaka Garden, Wooded Island, Jackson Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1893, Japan built its Phoenix Pavilion (Ho-o-den) on the Wooded Island for the World’s Fair.

  2. ★

    After the fair, Japan gave the Phoenix Pavilion to the City of Chicago.

  3. ★

    In 1935, a new Japanese garden opened beside it, with ponds, a waterfall, a moon bridge and stone lanterns.

  4. ★

    The garden was later named the Osaka Garden to honor Chicago’s sister city, Osaka.

  5. ★

    Jackson Park, where the garden sits, was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1972.
