THE SOUTH SHORE CLUB

Chicago turned a private country club into a public palace on the lake.

◉ South Shore Cultural Center (old South Shore Country Club) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Lawrence Heyworth founded South Shore Country Club in 1905 as an in-city lake retreat

  2. ★

    Marshall & Fox; a larger Mediterranean Revival clubhouse opened in 1916

  3. ★

    Park District bought the grounds in 1974; National Register listing in 1975

  4. ★

    In 2004 the City named it a Chicago Landmark

  5. ★

    Today: beach, nine-hole golf, gardens, and a nature sanctuary
