THE GREEN CHICAGO RIVER

Every March, Chicago turns its river bright green — using a powder that’s orange.

◉ Chicago River at Michigan Avenue (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The river was first dyed green in 1962.

  2. ★

    The idea came from a plumbers’ leak-detection dye that stained a worker’s coveralls.

  3. ★

    That first year, 100 pounds of dye kept it green for a whole week.

  4. ★

    The dye goes in as an orange powder and turns green in the water.

  5. ★

    Two families have dyed it for decades and keep the recipe secret.
