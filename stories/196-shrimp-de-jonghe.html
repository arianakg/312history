SHRIMP DE JONGHE

This classic Chicago dish is shrimp baked with garlic, butter, and breadcrumbs

◉ Monroe Street, the Loop (former De Jonghe's Hotel and Restaurant) (map pin approximate)

Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    This classic Chicago dish is shrimp baked with garlic, butter, and breadcrumbs

  2. ★

    It was first served at De Jonghe's Hotel and Restaurant, in the Loop

  3. ★

    Belgian brothers opened a restaurant in Chicago around the 1893 World's Fair

  4. ★

    In 1923 the hotel was shut for breaking Prohibition laws, and it did not reopen

  5. ★

    Chicago restaurants still serve Shrimp De Jonghe today
