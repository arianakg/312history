★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1908, Salvatore Ferrara opened a pastry and candy shop in Chicago's Little Italy

  2. ★

    In 1954, his son Nello introduced Atomic Fireballs

  3. ★

    In 1959, the candy works moved to Forest Park

  4. ★

    In 1962, Nello launched Lemonheads

  5. ★

    The factory is still there, at 7301 Harrison Street
