THE CHICAGO RIVERWALK

Chicago built a walkway under its downtown bridges, right at the water's edge.

◉ Chicago Riverwalk (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The final section of the Riverwalk opened in October 2016.

  2. ★

    That completed a 1.25-mile walkway along the river through downtown.

  3. ★

    The new stretch is split into "rooms," each with its own design and purpose.

  4. ★

    The River Theater is a wide set of steps from Upper Wacker Drive down to the water.

  5. ★

    One room, the Cove, offers kayak rentals right on the river.
