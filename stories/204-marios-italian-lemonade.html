MARIO'S ITALIAN LEMONADE

This Chicago stand has sold Italian lemonade since 1954

◉ 1068 W. Taylor St., Little Italy, Chicago (Mario's Italian Lemonade) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    This Chicago stand has sold Italian lemonade since 1954

  2. ★

    It sits on Taylor Street, in Little Italy

  3. ★

    In 1954, six-year-old Skip DiPaolo sold cups here for two cents

  4. ★

    The same family still runs the stand today

  5. ★

    Find it at 1068 West Taylor Street, in the warm months. It usually opens in May
