MARSHALL FIELD'S

A Great Clock has stood at State and Washington since 1907

◉ 111 N. State St., Chicago (former Marshall Field's) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    A Great Clock has stood at State and Washington since 1907

  2. ★

    In 1907, a Tiffany mosaic ceiling was set inside the store

  3. ★

    The Walnut Room opened that same year. A Great Tree still stands there at Christmas

  4. ★

    The store's famous line was, "Give the lady what she wants."

  5. ★

    In 2006, the store became Macy's. The clock and the ceiling are still there
