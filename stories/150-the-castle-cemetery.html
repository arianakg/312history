THE CASTLE CEMETERY

Why does this Chicago cemetery have a CASTLE gate?

◉ Rosehill Cemetery, Lincoln Square (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Rosehill Cemetery opened in 1859 on Chicago’s North Side.

  2. ★

    Landscape gardener William Saunders laid it out with winding roads, ponds and groves.

  3. ★

    Its castle-like gate of 1864 was designed by W. W. Boyington, who later designed the Water Tower.

  4. ★

    Legend says the name comes from “Roe’s Hill,” after local tavern keeper Hiram Roe, but it’s unproven.

  5. ★

    Mail-order rivals Richard Sears and Aaron Montgomery Ward are both buried there.
