FOUNTAIN OF THE GREAT LAKES

This Chicago fountain is a secret map of the Great Lakes.

◉ Fountain of the Great Lakes, Art Institute (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Lorado Taft's fountain was completed in 1913

  2. ★

    Its five bronze women stand for the five Great Lakes

  3. ★

    Water spills shell to shell, Superior down to Ontario

  4. ★

    It began as a class project by five of Taft's women students

  5. ★

    1963: moved and turned west for the Art Institute's Morton Wing

Full source list
  • Completed 1913, Lorado Taft — Chicago Park District (chicagoparkdistrict.com/parks-facilities/fountain-great-lakes-artwork) + SAIC news (saic.edu/news/class-captures-lorado-tafts-fountain-great-lakes, "1907-1913") + CAC ("Fountain of Great Lakes, 1913").
  • Five women = five Great Lakes — Park District + SAIC + CAC.
  • Water passes shell to shell, Superior to Ontario, mirroring the lakes — SAIC (Superior at top to Ontario at base) + Park District (each spills water to the next, emulating the lakes' flow).
  • Began as class assignment; five women students modeled figures — Chicago Architecture Center ("5 pieces of Chicago art") + "Lorado Taft: The Prairie State Sculptor" research blog (names Nellie Walker, Angelica McNulty, Clara Leonard, Lily Schoenbrun, Edith Parker; spring 1902). FLAG: second source is a blog; artandarchitecture-sf.com agrees.
  • Moved/reoriented in 1963 for Morton Wing — Park District + SAIC (+ Taft blog).
