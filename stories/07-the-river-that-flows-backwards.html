THE RIVER THAT FLOWS BACKWARDS

Chicago's sewers once drained into the Chicago River, and the river emptied into Lake Michigan, the city's drinking water. After a huge storm in 1885 washed river filth far into the lake, the fear of disease was enough: in 1889 the state created the Sanitary District.

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    (About that "90,000 people died in 1885" story you may have heard: it's a myth. No such epidemic happened.)

  2. ★

    The fix was bold. Engineers dug the 28-mile Sanitary and Ship Canal, at the time the largest earth-moving project in North America. In January 1900 it opened, and the river reversed, sending the sewage inland toward the Illinois and Mississippi rivers, away from the lake.

  3. ★

    Downstream wasn't thrilled: Missouri sued, and in 1906 the U.S. Supreme Court ruled for Illinois. In 1999 civil engineers named the system a "Monument of the Millennium."

  4. ★

    And since 1962, the plumbers union has dyed the river green every year for St. Patrick's Day.
