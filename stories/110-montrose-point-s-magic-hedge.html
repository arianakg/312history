MONTROSE POINT’S MAGIC HEDGE

Each spring and fall, migrating birds stop to rest and feed at Montrose Point on Chicago’s lakefront.

◉ Montrose Point Bird Sanctuary, Lincoln Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    More than 300 bird species have been recorded at Montrose Point.

  2. ★

    The bird sanctuary covers 15 acres of Lincoln Park.

  3. ★

    A row of shrubs and trees there, the “Magic Hedge,” is known for drawing many migrating birds.

  4. ★

    The sanctuary sits on a small point of land that reaches out into Lake Michigan.

  5. ★

    Its habitats include grassland, wildflowers, trees and shrubs.
