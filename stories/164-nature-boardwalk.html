NATURE BOARDWALK

A giant wooden turtle shell frames Chicago's skyline.

◉ Nature Boardwalk, Lincoln Park Zoo (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Opened June 24, 2010, at Lincoln Park Zoo

  2. ★

    Remade the zoo's old man-made South Pond into a prairie wetland

  3. ★

    About 14 acres of native habitat

  4. ★

    Studio Gang's pavilion: curved laminated-wood ribs + fiberglass pods

  5. ★

    Night herons and turtles now live here

Full source list
  • Opened June 24, 2010 — Lincoln Park Zoo (lpzoo.org/pressroom/nature-boardwalk) + Studio Gang (completed 2010) + Chicago Architecture Center (completed 2010) + Architectural Record (completion June 2010).
  • Former man-made South Pond remade as native prairie wetland — LPZ ("former manmade pond", Midwestern prairie ecosystem) + Studio Gang (19th/early-20th c. South Pond turned ecological habitat).
  • ~14 acres — Studio Gang (14 acres) + CAC (14-acre park); LPZ gives 609,840 sq ft = 14.0 acres.
  • Pavilion of curved prefabricated laminated-wood ribs + fiberglass pods/domes — Architectural Record (May 16 2011) + Studio Gang.
  • Night herons and turtles — LPZ (black-crowned night herons nesting, turtles) + CAC (night heron, turtles).
