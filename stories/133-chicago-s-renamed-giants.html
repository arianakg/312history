CHICAGO'S RENAMED GIANTS

Chicago's tallest towers keep changing their names. Do you keep up?

◉ Renamed skyscrapers, the Loop and Streeterville (map pin approximate)

Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Sears Tower officially became Willis Tower on July 16, 2009, after 36 years under the Sears name.

  2. ★

    Aon Center opened in 1973 as the Standard Oil Building. It became the Amoco Building in 1985, then Aon Center.

  3. ★

    Its thin Italian marble cracked and bowed in Chicago weather, so from 1990 to 1992 it was refaced in granite.

  4. ★

    The new skin cost about $80 million. Old marble was crushed for landscaping at an Amoco refinery in Indiana.

  5. ★

    In 2018 the John Hancock Center lost its name and became 875 North Michigan Avenue, its street address.
