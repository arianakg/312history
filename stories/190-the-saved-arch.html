THE SAVED ARCH

A Chicago skyscraper was torn down. Its entrance still stands

◉ Art Institute of Chicago (Stock Exchange Arch, Columbus Dr. & Monroe St.) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    A Chicago skyscraper was torn down. Its entrance still stands

  2. ★

    Adler and Sullivan designed the 13-story Chicago Stock Exchange

  3. ★

    The building at 30 North LaSalle Street was demolished in 1972

  4. ★

    In 1977, its terra-cotta arch was installed at the Art Institute

  5. ★

    You can still walk through the rebuilt trading room today
