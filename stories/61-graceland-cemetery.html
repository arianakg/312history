GRACELAND CEMETERY

Founded in 1860, Graceland is a park-like cemetery where some of Chicago's greatest architects rest.

◉ Uptown (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Businessman Thomas Bryan founded Graceland in 1860 as a private, park-like cemetery just north of the city limits.

  2. ★

    When the old City Cemetery on the lakefront was closed, many graves were moved to Graceland. That land became Lincoln Park, where the Couch Tomb still stands.

  3. ★

    The Getty Tomb, designed by Louis Sullivan in 1890 for Carrie Eliza Getty, became a Chicago Landmark in 1971.

  4. ★

    Lorado Taft's hooded bronze figure "Eternal Silence" (1909) marks the grave of early Chicago hotel keeper Dexter Graves.

  5. ★

    Louis Sullivan, Daniel Burnham and Mies van der Rohe are buried here. Burnham's grave is a boulder on the small island in Lake Willowmere.
