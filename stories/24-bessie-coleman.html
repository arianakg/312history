BESSIE COLEMAN

No flight school in America would teach her. So she learned in France.

◉ Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Born in Texas in 1892, she moved to Chicago and worked as a manicurist.

  2. ★

    Flight schools in the US turned her away because she was Black and a woman.

  3. ★

    Chicago Defender publisher Robert S. Abbott backed her. She trained at Le Crotoy in France.

  4. ★

    On June 15, 1921, she got an international pilot's license — the first African American woman, and the first Native American, to hold one.

  5. ★

    Known as "Queen Bess," she thrilled crowds at air shows. She died in a plane crash in 1926, at 34.
