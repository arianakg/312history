1889

JANE ADDAMS & HULL HOUSE

A house on the Near West Side changed how America helps its neighbors.

◉ Near West Side (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    In September 1889, Jane Addams and Ellen Gates Starr moved into an old mansion and opened Hull House.

  2. ★

    Most neighbors were new immigrants. They came for kindergarten, classes, clubs, music and art.

  3. ★

    Hull House grew into a complex of 13 buildings, with a gym, a theater and a coffee house.

  4. ★

    In 1931 Jane Addams became the first American woman to win the Nobel Peace Prize.

Myth or fact? "Jane Addams built Hull House." Myth. The house was built in 1856 for Charles Hull. Addams and Starr moved in and opened it to the whole neighborhood. Today it is the Jane Addams Hull-House Museum.
