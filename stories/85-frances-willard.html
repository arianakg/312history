FRANCES WILLARD

An Evanston teacher became the first woman honored with a statue in the U.S. Capitol's Statuary Hall.

◉ Frances Willard House, Evanston (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1858 Frances Willard came to Evanston to study at North Western Female College. She graduated in 1859.

  2. ★

    In 1871 she became president of the new Evanston College for Ladies.

  3. ★

    When it merged with Northwestern University in 1873, she became the university's first Dean of Women. She resigned in 1874.

  4. ★

    In 1879 she was elected president of the national Woman's Christian Temperance Union. She was re-elected every year until her death in 1898.

  5. ★

    In 1905 Illinois placed her statue in the U.S. Capitol. It was the first statue of a woman in National Statuary Hall.
