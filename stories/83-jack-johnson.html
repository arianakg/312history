JACK JOHNSON

The first Black heavyweight boxing champion opened a Chicago nightclub, then a Chicago jury convicted him.

◉ Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    On December 26, 1908, in Sydney, Australia, Jack Johnson beat Tommy Burns to become the first Black world heavyweight boxing champion.

  2. ★

    On July 4, 1910, in Reno, Nevada, he beat Jim Jeffries, a retired champion who came back to fight him.

  3. ★

    In July 1912 Johnson opened the Café de Champion in Chicago, a nightclub open to both Black and white guests.

  4. ★

    In May 1913 an all-white jury in Chicago convicted him under the Mann Act. The case is widely seen as racially motivated.

  5. ★

    On May 24, 2018, more than 70 years after his death, Johnson received a posthumous presidential pardon.
