RADIO FLYER

The original little red wagon was first made in Chicago

◉ West Grand Avenue, Belmont-Cragin, Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The original little red wagon was first made in Chicago

  2. ★

    In 1917, Antonio Pasin began building wooden wagons in Chicago. He called them Liberty Coasters

  3. ★

    He later made steel wagons and named one the Radio Flyer, after radios and airplanes

  4. ★

    At the 1933 World's Fair, a 45-foot boy in a wagon helped make the name famous

  5. ★

    The company is still based in Chicago today
