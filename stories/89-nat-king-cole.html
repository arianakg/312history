NAT KING COLE

Before the world knew his voice, Nat King Cole was a kid growing up on Chicago's South Side.

◉ South Side (map pin approximate)

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Nathaniel Adams Coles was born in Montgomery, Alabama, on March 17, 1919.

  2. ★

    As a small child he moved with his family to Chicago, where his father became a Baptist minister.

  3. ★

    In 1943 his trio recorded "Straighten Up and Fly Right." It became a hit in 1944.

  4. ★

    On November 5, 1956, NBC launched The Nat "King" Cole Show. It never found a national sponsor and ended in December 1957.

  5. ★

    He died on February 15, 1965, at age 45.
