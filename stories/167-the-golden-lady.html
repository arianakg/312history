THE GOLDEN LADY

Chicago's Golden Lady is a mini copy of a 65-foot giant

◉ Statue of the Republic, Jackson Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The 1893 World's Fair's "Republic" stood 65 ft over the Court of Honor

  2. ★

    It was temporary plaster, destroyed after the fair

  3. ★

    In 1918 Daniel Chester French made a 24-ft gilded bronze, 1/3 size

  4. ★

    It stands where the fair's Administration Building stood

  5. ★

    French later sculpted the Lincoln Memorial's seated Lincoln
