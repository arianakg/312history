CHICAGO TODAY: ONE OF A KIND

From a muddy portage to a world city. Today Chicago is the third-largest city in the US, with about 2.7 million people.

◉ Chicago today (map pin approximate)

Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    Willis Tower: 1,451 ft and 110 stories, the world's tallest building when it opened in 1973.

  2. ★

    The 'L': the first elevated line opened in 1892, and part of it still runs as today's Green Line.

  3. ★

    Deep dish: first served in 1943 at the spot now called Pizzeria Uno. Who invented it? Still debated.

  4. ★

    26 miles of Lake Michigan shoreline, mostly public parks and free beaches.
