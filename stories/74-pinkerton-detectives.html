1842

PINKERTON DETECTIVES

A Chicago barrel maker became the detective who helped get Lincoln safely to Washington.

◉ Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Allan Pinkerton came from Scotland in 1842 and worked as a barrel maker (cooper) in Chicago and Dundee, Illinois.

  2. ★

    Around 1850 he started a private detective agency in Chicago. Its motto was "We Never Sleep."

  3. ★

    In February 1861, his agents on a railroad case found a suspected plot to kill President-elect Lincoln in Baltimore. Lincoln's route was changed so he passed through the city at night.

  4. ★

    When the Civil War began, General George McClellan, who knew him from the Illinois Central Railroad, hired Pinkerton to run army intelligence. He signed his reports "E. J. Allen."

  5. ★

    He returned to running his agency from Chicago, wrote popular true-crime books, and died in Chicago in 1884. He is buried at Graceland Cemetery.
