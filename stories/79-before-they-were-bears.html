1920

BEFORE THEY WERE BEARS

Chicago's football team began as a starch company's squad in Decatur.

◉ Cubs Park (Wrigley Field) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1920 the A.E. Staley starch company in Decatur, Illinois, sponsored a football team, the Decatur Staleys, with George Halas running it.

  2. ★

    On September 17, 1920, at a meeting in Canton, Ohio, the Staleys, represented by Halas, joined the new American Professional Football Association. In 1922 it was renamed the National Football League.

  3. ★

    In 1921 the Staley company handed Halas the team and $5,000, on the condition that he keep the Staleys name for a year. He moved the team to Cubs Park in Chicago, and the league named the Staleys its 1921 champions.

  4. ★

    In 1922 the Chicago Staleys became the Chicago Bears.

  5. ★

    On Thanksgiving Day 1925, Illinois star Red Grange played his first game with the Bears. About 36,000 people watched a scoreless tie with the Chicago Cardinals at Wrigley Field.
