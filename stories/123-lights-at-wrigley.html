LIGHTS AT WRIGLEY

On 8/8/88, Wrigley Field finally played under the lights — and then the rain came.

◉ Wrigley Field, Lakeview (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Wrigley Field’s first night game was played on August 8, 1988.

  2. ★

    That night, the lights were switched on at 6:09 p.m.

  3. ★

    The Cubs were the last Major League team to add lights to their ballpark.

  4. ★

    Rain stopped that first night game in the fourth inning.

  5. ★

    The next night, the Cubs beat the Mets 6–4 in the first official night game.
