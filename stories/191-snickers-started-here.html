SNICKERS STARTED HERE

This famous candy bar was first made in Chicago

◉ 2019 N. Oak Park Ave. (former Mars Candy Factory, Galewood) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    This famous candy bar was first made in Chicago

  2. ★

    Mars moved its candy company from Minneapolis to Chicago in 1929

  3. ★

    They built a factory in Galewood, at 2019 North Oak Park Avenue

  4. ★

    Snickers came out in 1930. It was named after a Mars family horse

  5. ★

    The Milky Way bar came first, in 1923

Full source list
  • Mars, Incorporated, “Our History.” https://www.mars.com/about/history
  • 1923: Frank introduces the MILKY WAY bar after a “malted milk in a candy bar” idea from a discussion with his son Forrest Sr.
  • 1929: “Mars, Incorporated relocates and opens a full-production plant in Chicago. Close to 200 Associates move with Mars.”
  • 1930: “The SNICKERS brand is launched.”
  • The official timeline does not mention the horse name.
  • SNICKERS, “Our Story.” https://www.snickers.com/our-story
  • 1930: “The very first SNICKERS Bar, allegedly named after a horse, was manufactured in Chicago and sold for five cents.”
  • Commission on Chicago Landmarks, *Mars Candy Factory, 2019 North Oak Park Avenue*, Final Designation Report, 11 July 2024. PDF: https://reports.chicagolandmarkreports.org/mars-candy-factory-l392.pdf (same report also at https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/zlup/Historic_Preservat…
  • Address 2019 North Oak Park Avenue; construction 1928–1929; opened 1929; first Mars plant built outside Minneapolis.
  • Quotes the Chicago Tribune of 12 August 1928 announcing the move from Minneapolis to a plant “now under construction at 2019-59 North Oak Park Avenue.”
  • Milky Way invented in 1923, before the move; Snickers developed in 1930 and “invented and first produced at the Chicago factory.”
  • “Named after one of Frank’s favorite horses at his Milky Way Farm in Tennessee.”
  • As of the July 2024 report: the factory “is still in use but will permanently close in 2024.”
