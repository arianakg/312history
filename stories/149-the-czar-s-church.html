THE CZAR’S CHURCH

A Russian CZAR helped pay for this Chicago church?

◉ Holy Trinity Cathedral, Ukrainian Village (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Holy Trinity Cathedral in Ukrainian Village was designed by Louis Sullivan and finished in 1903.

  2. ★

    Russia’s Czar Nicholas II gave about $4,000 toward building it.

  3. ★

    Sullivan shaped it after the small Russian provincial churches its first members knew.

  4. ★

    Nearby, St. Nicholas Ukrainian Catholic Cathedral, completed in 1915, was modeled on St. Sophia in Kyiv.

  5. ★

    Its 13 domes stand for Christ and the 12 apostles.
