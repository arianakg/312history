CHICAGO'S 5 TALLEST BUILDINGS

Can you name Chicago's five tallest buildings, in order?

◉ Chicago skyline, the Loop and Streeterville (map pin approximate)

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    No. 5, 875 North Michigan Avenue, about 1,128 ft: offices below, homes above, starting on the 46th floor.

  2. ★

    No. 4, Aon Center, 1,136 ft: its architect gave Toronto a near-twin, First Canadian Place.

  3. ★

    No. 3, St. Regis Chicago, about 1,191 ft: it is built from frustums, pyramids with the tips cut off.

  4. ★

    No. 2, Trump International Hotel & Tower, about 1,389 ft: its first setback lines up with the Wrigley Building.

  5. ★

    No. 1, Willis Tower: nine square tubes bundled together, ending at different heights to make its steps.
