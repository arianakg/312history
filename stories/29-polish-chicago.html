POLISH CHICAGO

One of the great Polish cities outside Poland grew up around a Chicago church.

◉ Polish Downtown (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Polish Downtown grew around the Polish Triangle, where Division, Ashland and Milwaukee meet. In 1867, St Stanislaus Kostka became Chicago's first Polish parish.

  2. ★

    The biggest Polish wave came from the 1850s to the early 1920s. Many came "za chlebem" — for bread, work and a better life.

  3. ★

    By 1900, Chicago had many Polish Catholic parishes, plus Polish schools and newspapers like Dziennik Chicagoski, founded in 1890.

  4. ★

    By 1930, Polish immigrants and their children were one of Chicago's largest ethnic groups.

  5. ★

    The Polish Museum of America was founded in 1935 and opened to the public in 1937. Illinois made the first Monday in March Casimir Pulaski Day in 1977, first celebrated statewide in 1978.
