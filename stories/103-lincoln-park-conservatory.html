LINCOLN PARK CONSERVATORY

Built from 1890 to 1895, Lincoln Park Conservatory still grows exotic plants under glass.

◉ Lincoln Park Conservatory, Lincoln Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    It was built in phases between 1890 and 1895, replacing an earlier greenhouse.

  2. ★

    It is a Victorian-era glass house with an iron-and-glass structure.

  3. ★

    It has four display houses: the Palm House, Fern Room, Orchid House and Show House.

  4. ★

    It was designed both to show exotic plants and to grow plants for use in the parks.

  5. ★

    Admission is free.
