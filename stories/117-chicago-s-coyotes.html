CHICAGO’S COYOTES

Thousands of wild coyotes live in the Chicago area — some even downtown.

◉ Coyotes in Lincoln Park, Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Researchers have tracked Chicago-area coyotes since 2000.

  2. ★

    They eat mostly natural prey like rodents, rarely garbage or pets.

  3. ★

    Cars are the biggest danger they face.

  4. ★

    Their mating season peaks in February, around Valentine’s Day.

  5. ★

    The area’s coyote population has grown since the 2000s.
