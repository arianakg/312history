THE CHRISTKINDLMARKET

Chicago’s Christkindlmarket is a free, German-style outdoor holiday market.

◉ Christkindlmarket, Chicago Loop (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    It was first held in 1996.

  2. ★

    The first market had just 13 vendors.

  3. ★

    It began as a German-American trade initiative.

  4. ★

    Its hot spiced wine, Glühwein, literally means “glow wine.”

  5. ★

    It has drawn more than 1.5 million visitors in a season.
