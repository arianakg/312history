100 TONS OF BELLS

A Chicago tower hides 100 tons of bells?

◉ Rockefeller Chapel, Hyde Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The University of Chicago's chapel was completed in 1928, designed by architect Bertram Grosvenor Goodhue.

  2. ★

    Its stone tower rises 207 feet above the Midway Plaisance.

  3. ★

    In 1932 a carillon of 72 bells was installed in the tower.

  4. ★

    Together the bells weigh more than 100 tons of bronze.

  5. ★

    First called the University Chapel, it was renamed Rockefeller Memorial Chapel in 1937.
