A TOWER FELL AT THE WORLD'S FAIR

On July 10, 1893, a building at Chicago's World's Fair caught fire. Firefighters climbed a tall wooden tower. The fire cut off their way down, and the tower fell.

◉ Jackson Park (World's Columbian Exposition grounds) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    On July 10, 1893, the Cold Storage Building at the World's Columbian Exposition caught fire

  2. ★

    The building kept the fair's food cold and also had an ice rink

  3. ★

    A tall wooden tower hid a smokestack; firefighters climbed the tower

  4. ★

    The fire cut off their way down, and the tower fell

  5. ★

    Seventeen people died; thousands at the fair saw it

Photos: B.W. Kilburn, 1893 (PD); Stone, Kastler & Painter, 1893 (PD); J.W. Buel, The Magic City, 1894 (PD). Music: original piano.
