LICENSE NO. 1

One of Chicago's oldest restaurants opened in 1898

◉ 17 W. Adams St. (The Berghoff) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    One of Chicago's oldest restaurants opened in 1898

  2. ★

    Herman Berghoff sold beer at the 1893 World's Fair

  3. ★

    They stayed open with near beer and root beer

  4. ★

    After Prohibition, they got Chicago's liquor license No. 1

  5. ★

    It is still open at 17 West Adams Street
