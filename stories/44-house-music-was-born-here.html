HOUSE MUSIC WAS BORN HERE

A Chicago club called the Warehouse gave a whole genre its name.

◉ West Loop (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    From 1977 to 1982, a Chicago dance club called the Warehouse was the cradle of a new sound. The City of Chicago calls the building the birthplace of house music, and made it a landmark in 2023.

  2. ★

    Frankie Knuckles, a DJ from New York, was the Warehouse’s resident DJ from 1977 to 1982. Dancers called him the Godfather of House Music.

  3. ★

    Knuckles rebuilt songs for the dance floor: he extended and re-edited disco, R&B, soul and gospel records, and added a steady, driving beat underneath.

  4. ★

    The name stuck to the club. Most music historians link "house" to the Warehouse — Knuckles recalled first seeing a sign, around 1980 or 1981, that said: "We play house music."

  5. ★

    Then the sound reached vinyl. Jesse Saunders’ "On & On," recorded in 1983 and released in early 1984, is often cited as the first house record released on vinyl.
