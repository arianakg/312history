BABY RUTH STARTED HERE

A Chicago company made this candy bar in 1921. Otto Schnering's Curtiss Candy Company sold it for a nickel. They dropped candy from planes on little parachutes. The company said it was named for a president's daughter. Many people thought of the baseball star Babe Ruth.
