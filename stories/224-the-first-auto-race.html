THE FIRST AUTO RACE

Did you know America's first auto race was in Chicago?

◉ Midway Plaisance at Jackson Park, Chicago (start and finish; near today's Museum of Science and Industry) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Did you know America's first auto race was in Chicago?

  2. ★

    It was run on Thanksgiving Day in 1895, in snow and slush

  3. ★

    The cars started at the Midway, by Jackson Park, and drove to Evanston and back

  4. ★

    Only six cars started. Only two finished

  5. ★

    J. Frank Duryea won. The trip took more than 10 hours
