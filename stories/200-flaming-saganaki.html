FLAMING SAGANAKI

This flaming cheese started in Chicago, not in Greece

◉ 314 S. Halsted St., Greektown, Chicago (former site of The Parthenon) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    This flaming cheese started in Chicago, not in Greece

  2. ★

    In Greece, saganaki is fried cheese, usually served without a flame

  3. ★

    In Chicago, a waiter pours brandy on the hot cheese, lights it, and shouts Opa!

  4. ★

    The tradition took off in Greektown in 1968. The Parthenon restaurant is widely credited

  5. ★

    The Parthenon later closed, but Chicago restaurants still serve it
