THE WEBER KETTLE

Did you know the Weber kettle was invented in Chicago?

◉ 108 N. Jefferson St., Chicago (former Weber Brothers Metal Works) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Did you know the Weber kettle was invented in Chicago?

  2. ★

    In 1952, George Stephen worked at a metal shop on Jefferson Street in Chicago

  3. ★

    The shop made metal buoys. He used two halves to build a grill with a lid

  4. ★

    He called it George's Barbecue Kettle. The lid helped him control the heat

  5. ★

    The kettle became a classic in American backyards
