THE TREE MUSEUM

The Arbor Day family built THIS?

◉ Morton Arboretum, Lisle (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Morton Arboretum in Lisle was founded in 1922 by Joy Morton, head of Morton Salt.

  2. ★

    His father, J. Sterling Morton, created Arbor Day in Nebraska in 1872.

  3. ★

    It began as a 175-acre estate and has grown to about 1,700 acres.

  4. ★

    Its herbarium holds about 165,000 preserved plant specimens.

  5. ★

    In 1962, it started one of the Chicago region’s first prairie restorations, the Schulenberg Prairie.
