THE TEMPLE OF LIGHT

It took 41 YEARS to build this?

◉ Bahá’í House of Worship, Wilmette (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Bahá’í House of Worship in Wilmette had its cornerstone laid in 1912 and was dedicated in 1953, 41 years later.

  2. ★

    The building has nine sides.

  3. ★

    Its lacy white skin is cast concrete mixed with crushed quartz, made by John Earley’s studio.

  4. ★

    Architect Louis Bourgeois died in 1930 and never saw the temple finished.

  5. ★

    It is the only Bahá’í House of Worship in the United States.
