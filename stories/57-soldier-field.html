SOLDIER FIELD

Opened in 1924 on the lakefront, the stadium was renamed a year later to honor America's fallen soldiers.

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The South Park Commission hired architects Holabird & Roche in 1919. The stadium opened on October 9, 1924, as Municipal Grant Park Stadium.

  2. ★

    On November 11, 1925, at the urging of Chicago's Gold Star Mothers, it was renamed Soldier Field, as a memorial to U.S. soldiers who died in World War I.

  3. ★

    In November 1926, more than 100,000 people watched Army and Navy play at Soldier Field. The game ended in a 21–21 tie.

  4. ★

    In September 1927, about 104,000 fans saw the Jack Dempsey–Gene Tunney rematch, famous for the "long count." Tunney kept his heavyweight title.

  5. ★

    Its double rows of Doric columns became its signature. Soldier Field was named a National Historic Landmark in 1987; the status was withdrawn in 2006 after the 2003 rebuild.
