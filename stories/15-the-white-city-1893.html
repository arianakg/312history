THE WHITE CITY, 1893

For six months Chicago built a dream city by the lake. Then most of it vanished.

◉ Jackson Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    The World's Columbian Exposition filled about 600 acres of Jackson Park. Chicago beat New York, Washington and St. Louis to host it.

  2. ★

    Under director of works Daniel Burnham, grand Beaux-Arts halls were covered in plaster and painted white: the "White City."

  3. ★

    Open May 1 – Oct 30, 1893: 27,529,400 admissions in 179 days.

  4. ★

    The Palace of Fine Arts became the Field Museum's first home and was later rebuilt as today's Museum of Science and Industry.

Myth or fact? "H. H. Holmes murdered hundreds of fair visitors." Myth. Historians link him to about nine murders and find no evidence he killed fair-goers. The "murder castle" tales came from 1890s tabloids.
