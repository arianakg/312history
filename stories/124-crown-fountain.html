CROWN FOUNTAIN

In 2004, Millennium Park got two glass towers with giant faces that spit water.

◉ Crown Fountain, Millennium Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Crown Fountain debuted with Millennium Park in 2004.

  2. ★

    Its two glass-brick towers stand 50 feet tall.

  3. ★

    The towers show the faces of about 1,000 Chicagoans.

  4. ★

    Water spouts from the faces’ mouths, like a modern gargoyle.

  5. ★

    The water on its granite pool is only 1/8 inch deep, so visitors can walk on it.
