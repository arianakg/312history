1892

THE MASONIC TEMPLE

In 1892, a 300-foot giant with steep gabled roofs rose over State Street. By 1939 it was gone.

◉ State & Randolph (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Masonic Temple, designed by Burnham and Root, was completed in 1892 at the northeast corner of State and Randolph streets.

  2. ★

    It stood about 302 feet tall. Sources still disagree on its stories, counting 20, 21, or 22 depending on the attic.

  3. ★

    At the top was a glass-roofed garden and observatory where, on a clear day, visitors were said to see four states.

  4. ★

    Shops ringed its skylit central court on the lower floors, with offices above and Masonic halls at the top.

  5. ★

    Later called the Capitol Building, it was seen as old-fashioned and was demolished in 1939.
