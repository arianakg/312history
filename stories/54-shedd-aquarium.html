SHEDD AQUARIUM

Opened in 1930, it brought the ocean to Chicago — by train.

◉ Museum Campus (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    John G. Shedd rose from the stockroom to president of Marshall Field & Company. He gave about $3 million to build an aquarium for Chicago.

  2. ★

    Shedd died in October 1926 and saw only the architect's first drawings. Ground was broken on November 2, 1927, for a Beaux-Arts building by Graham, Anderson, Probst & White.

  3. ★

    The aquarium officially opened on May 30, 1930. Shedd's widow, Mary R. Shedd, cut the ribbon.

  4. ★

    It was the first inland aquarium with a permanent saltwater collection. In 1930, railroad tank cars brought about one million gallons of seawater from Key West to Chicago.

  5. ★

    The Abbott Oceanarium opened in 1991, faced with the same white Georgia marble as the original building. The Shedd is now a National Historic Landmark.
