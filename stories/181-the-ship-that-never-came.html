THE SHIP THAT NEVER CAME

Nov. 23, 1912: Chicago’s Christmas tree ship vanished on the lake.

◉ Clark Street docks / Lake Michigan (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The schooner Rouse Simmons sold trees at the Clark Street docks

  2. ★

    Papers called Capt. Herman Schuenemann “Captain Santa”

  3. ★

    On Nov. 22, 1912, she left Michigan with about 3,000–5,000 trees

  4. ★

    A Wisconsin lookout saw a distress flag — then snow hid the ship

  5. ★

    All hands were lost. The wreck was found in 1971, trees still in the hold

Coast Guard Cutter Mackinaw brings trees to Navy Pier on Sat., Dec. 5, 2026 (unload 8:30 a.m.; ceremony 10 a.m.). Confirm on navypier.org.
