THE LEDGE

Since 2009, visitors can step into glass boxes hanging off the 103rd floor.

◉ The Ledge, Willis Tower Skydeck (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Ledge opened to the public at the Skydeck on July 2, 2009.

  2. ★

    Its glass boxes stick out a little more than 4 feet from the 103rd floor.

  3. ★

    Standing inside, you look 1,353 feet straight down to the street.

  4. ★

    Each box is made of three layers of half-inch glass, built to hold about 5 tons.

  5. ★

    In May 2014 a protective coating cracked underfoot. The glass itself did not.
