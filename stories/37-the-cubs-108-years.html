THE CUBS: 108 YEARS

They won in 1908. Then Chicago waited 108 years.

◉ Wrigleyville (map pin approximate)

Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Chicago Cubs won the World Series in 1907 and again in 1908. Then the wins stopped. For more than a century, Chicago fans watched other teams lift the trophy.

  2. ★

    The wait lasted 108 years — one of the longest title droughts in American sports — until the night of November 2, 2016, in Cleveland.

  3. ★

    That night the Cubs beat Cleveland 8–7 in 10 innings in Game 7 of the World Series, winning the series four games to three. A 108-year wait ended in one extra-inning game.

  4. ★

    Their home has its own long story. The ballpark on Chicago's North Side opened in 1914 as Weeghman Park. The Cubs moved in for the 1916 season, and the park took the Wrigley Field name in 1926.

  5. ★

    In 1937 the outfield was rebuilt, and two beloved sights arrived together: a big hand-operated scoreboard, still turned by hand today, and ivy planted at the base of the outfield wall. The ivy is a mix of Boston ivy and Japanese bittersweet.
