JULIUS ROSENWALD

A Sears boss helped build a Bronzeville landmark, a famous museum and nearly 5,000 schools.

◉ Wabash Avenue YMCA, Bronzeville (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1895 Chicago clothing merchant Julius Rosenwald bought a quarter of Sears, Roebuck & Co. for $37,500.

  2. ★

    The Wabash Avenue YMCA opened in Bronzeville in 1913, built with Rosenwald's money and funds raised by Black Chicagoans.

  3. ★

    In 1915 historian Carter G. Woodson founded the Association for the Study of Negro Life and History at the Wabash Avenue YMCA.

  4. ★

    Working with Booker T. Washington and Tuskegee Institute, Rosenwald helped Black communities build 4,977 schools in 15 Southern states by 1932.

  5. ★

    In 1926 he pledged $3 million for a Chicago science museum. He died in January 1932, and the Museum of Science and Industry first opened to visitors in June 1933.
