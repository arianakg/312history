THE AUDITORIUM BUILDING

Did you know a huge theatre, a hotel, and offices shared one building?

◉ The Auditorium Building, 430 S. Michigan Avenue, Chicago (The Loop)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Did you know a huge theatre, a hotel, and offices shared one building?

  2. ★

    In 1889, the Auditorium Building opened on Michigan Avenue

  3. ★

    Adler and Sullivan designed it

  4. ★

    The theatre had more than 4,000 seats

  5. ★

    It still stands today at 430 S. Michigan Avenue

PRIMARY SOURCES & CREDITS

Images: Photographs of the Auditorium Building by J. W. Taylor, 1890 (public domain). Cover and today frames: original 312 History engraving (no lettering). Music: original.

Full source list
  • City of Chicago Landmarks, Auditorium Building (landmark lanId 1246; designated September 15, 1976). Architects: Adler & Sullivan. The official page lists the building as 1886–1890 at 430 S. Michigan Avenue, and as a National Historic Landmark. https://webapps1.chicago.gov/landmarksweb/web/landmarkdetails.htm?lanId=1246
  • National Park Service, Auditorium Building, Roosevelt University. The place page says the building was completed in 1889 and that the theatre originally seated 4,200 people. National Historic Landmark, May 15, 1975; National Register of Historic Places, April 17, 1970. https://www.nps.gov/places/auditorium-roosevelt-u-il.htm
  • Chicago Architecture Center, Auditorium Building. Gives an original completion date of 1889 and names Adler & Sullivan as the architects. https://www.architecture.org/online-resources/buildings-of-chicago/auditorium-building
  • Encyclopedia of Chicago, "Auditorium Building." Discusses the mixed-use plan of a huge theatre with a hotel and offices, and notes that the hotel and office block were added in 1890. https://www.encyclopedia.chicagohistory.org/pages/89.html
  • National Register of Historic Places, Auditorium Building, NRIS 70000230.
