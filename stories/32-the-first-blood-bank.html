THE FIRST BLOOD BANK

The words "blood bank" were born in a Chicago hospital.

◉ Cook County Hospital (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    On March 15, 1937, Dr Bernard Fantus opened the first hospital blood bank in the United States at Cook County Hospital in Chicago, and coined the term "blood bank."

  2. ★

    Fantus was born in Hungary in 1874 and came to the United States in 1889. He earned his medical degree in 1899 and moved to Chicago in 1902.

  3. ★

    Before blood banks, a matching donor often had to be found and be there when a patient needed a transfusion. Stored blood meant help could be ready in advance.

  4. ★

    Fantus built on earlier work: Oswald Robertson used stored blood at aid stations in World War I, and Fantus credited Soviet researcher Sergei Yudin in his 1937 report. Fantus reported preserving blood for 10 days.

  5. ★

    Cook County says the blood bank cost $1,500 to open and helped with 1,354 transfusions in its first year. Storing blood for transfusion helped make modern surgery and emergency care possible.
