1891

MONADNOCK BUILDING

Its north half, finished in 1891, is said to be the last skyscraper held up by solid brick walls.

◉ Jackson Blvd (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Burnham & Root designed the north half, built in 1889–1891 on Jackson Boulevard. It rises 16 stories, about 215 feet.

  2. ★

    Its brick walls carry much of the weight and are six feet thick at the base. It is said to be the last skyscraper built with solid masonry walls.

  3. ★

    Its brick facade has almost no ornament, which was daring for its time.

  4. ★

    Its projecting bay windows, held up by cantilevered steel, bring extra daylight and floor space into the offices.

  5. ★

    Holabird & Roche added a steel-frame south half in 1893. The block was planned as four buildings named for New England mountains, including Monadnock. It became a Chicago Landmark in 1973.
