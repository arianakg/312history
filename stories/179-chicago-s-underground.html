CHICAGO’S UNDERGROUND

There’s a free city under the Loop. It’s called the Pedway.

◉ The Pedway, the Loop (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    Not a subway: tunnels and bridges linking downtown buildings

  2. ★

    Started in 1951 as short links between the Red and Blue Line subways

  3. ★

    City count: 40+ blocks, about 5 miles, 50+ buildings

  4. ★

    A 2022 photo shows a private club’s lap pool beside the Pedway (visibility may change)

Enter via the Cultural Center, Lake Red Line (188 N. State), or Washington Blue Line (19 N. Dearborn). Hours vary by door. Photos dated 2022.

PRIMARY SOURCES & CREDITS

Full source list
  • City of Chicago Pedway page https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/cdot/provdrs/ped/svcs/pedway.htm… — "system of underground tunnels and overhead bridges links more than 40 blocks … covering roughly five miles"; "connects more than 50 buildings"; "Development of the Pedway began in 1951, when the City of Chicago built one-block tunnels connecting the Red Line and Blue Line subways at Washington Street and Jackson Boulevard."
  • Chicago Architecture Center https://www.architecture.org/online-resources/buildings-of-chicago/chi… — corroborates 1951 Red/Blue links at Washington and Jackson; "five-mile labyrinth that connects more than 50 buildings".
  • City Pedway page does NOT publish a single closing time for the whole system. Official map PDF (Pedway System Map 1.6.2026) could not be downloaded from this environment (chicago.gov returned HTML block). Slides therefore say hours vary by segment; weekday daytime most reliable; check posted doors. Cultural Center building hours (Pedway elevators there) verified live: Daily 10 am–5 pm — https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/dca/supp_info/ccc.html ("An elevator connected to the underground Pedway is located in the Randolph Street Lobby").
  • Third-party tour pages often cite ~7 am–5 pm Mon–Fri; not used as an official claim on slides.
  • Lake (Red Line) https://www.transitchicago.com/station/lake/ — "Address: 188 N State St."; Subway; Accessible; notes Pedway transfer to Blue via Block 37; no station-closure alert (only unrelated Cermak-Chinatown elevator on Red Line).
  • Washington (Blue Line) https://www.transitchicago.com/station/wasb/ — "Address: 19 N. Dearborn St."; "Image - Entrance from Richard J. Daley Center via Pedway"; notes Pedway transfer to Red via Block 37; Washington-Madison mezzanine hours note (closed after 11 pm). Active Blue Line alert is O’Hare branch bus substitution west of Jefferson Park — does not close this station.
  • Explicitly NOT used: Washington Red (permanently gone; Red Line route Lake→Monroe→Jackson). State/Lake elevated closed into 2029 — https://www.transitchicago.com/travel-information/railstatus/ and https://www.transitchicago.com/station/stlk/.
  • Apr 21, 2022 photo (Aaron Volkening, CC BY 2.0) shows private club lap pool beside Pedway near Heritage Shops / Block 37.
  • LA Fitness East Loop (Signature), 55 E. Randolph St., still lists “Indoor Pool” on its live club page checked Nov 8, 2026: https://www.lafitness.com/Pages/clubhome.aspx?Chicago-Illinois=&cl…
  • Pedway glass-corridor visibility NOT confirmed by a Nov 2026 photo or official Pedway page. Slides/caption frame the image as a dated 2022 observation; cheat sheet no longer lists the pool as something to look for.
