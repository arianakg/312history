CHICAGO CABLE CARS

Long before the buses, Chicago rode to work on cars pulled by moving cables under the street.

◉ State St (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    On January 28, 1882, the Chicago City Railway started cable-car service on State Street, after studying San Francisco's system.

  2. ★

    Cable cars could not cross the river's movable bridges, so the lines used three tunnels under the Chicago River.

  3. ★

    On the North Side, cars reached the Loop through the LaSalle Street tunnel, pulled by cable from a powerhouse built in 1887.

  4. ★

    Chicago's lines grew to more than 80 miles, one of the largest cable systems in the world, carrying about 100,000 workers downtown a day.

  5. ★

    Electric streetcars began replacing cable in 1892, and the last cable car ran in 1906. The LaSalle Street powerhouse became a Chicago Landmark in 2001.
