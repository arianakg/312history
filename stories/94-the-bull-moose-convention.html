THE BULL MOOSE CONVENTION

In the summer of 1912, a new political party was launched at the Chicago Coliseum.

◉ Chicago Coliseum, Wabash Ave (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In June 1912, the Republican convention in Chicago renominated President William Howard Taft over Theodore Roosevelt.

  2. ★

    Roosevelt's supporters formed the Progressive Party, which met at the Chicago Coliseum on August 5–7, 1912.

  3. ★

    Roosevelt spoke to the delegates in person, giving a speech he called his "Confession of Faith."

  4. ★

    The convention nominated Roosevelt for president and California Governor Hiram Johnson for vice president.

  5. ★

    Nicknamed the "Bull Moose" party, it finished second in November, ahead of Taft; Woodrow Wilson won.
