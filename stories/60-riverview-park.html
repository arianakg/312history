RIVERVIEW PARK

For 64 summers, an amusement park at Belmont and Western was where Chicago went to play.

◉ Belmont & Western (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Riverview opened on July 2, 1904, at Belmont and Western avenues, on the grounds of a former German sharpshooters' park by the Chicago River.

  2. ★

    It opened with just a few attractions, among them a figure-eight roller coaster and a merry-go-round.

  3. ★

    Its most famous ride, the Bobs, opened in 1924: a wooden roller coaster with a drop of nearly 80 feet.

  4. ★

    Riverview grew into Chicago's largest and longest-running amusement park. In October 1967 it was sold, and it never reopened.

  5. ★

    Its 1908 carousel, built by the Philadelphia Toboggan Company, still turns at Six Flags Over Georgia and was added to the National Register in 1995.
