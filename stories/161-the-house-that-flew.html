THE HOUSE THAT FLEW

Chicago's oldest house once got stuck in the air over the L.

◉ Clarke House, Prairie Avenue District (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Built 1836 for merchant Henry B. Clarke

  2. ★

    Chicago's oldest surviving house

  3. ★

    Moved south to Wabash Ave. in the early 1870s

  4. ★

    1977: lifted over the L tracks to move back north

  5. ★

    The jacks froze; it hung there about two weeks

Leave it there, or move it home again?
