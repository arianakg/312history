FAMOUS CHICAGOANS

They're ALL from Chicago?

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Walt Disney was born in Chicago on December 5, 1901, in a house his father built in the Hermosa neighborhood.

  2. ★

    Ernest Hemingway was born on July 21, 1899, in Oak Park, the suburb just west of Chicago.

  3. ★

    The Oprah Winfrey Show was made in Chicago for 25 seasons, from 1986 to 2011.

  4. ★

    Quincy Jones was born in Chicago in 1933 and spent his early childhood on the South Side.

  5. ★

    At age 11, Herbie Hancock played Mozart with the Chicago Symphony Orchestra.
