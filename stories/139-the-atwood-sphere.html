THE ATWOOD SPHERE

Chicago's oldest planetarium is a giant METAL BALL?

◉ Adler Planetarium, Museum Campus (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Atwood Celestial Sphere opened in June 1913 at the Chicago Academy of Sciences, 17 years before the Adler Planetarium.

  2. ★

    It is a ball of thin galvanized sheet metal, about 15 feet across.

  3. ★

    Light shines through 692 holes in the metal, showing the brightest stars as seen from Chicago.

  4. ★

    Visitors sit inside while the sphere turns around them, so the stars wheel across the sky.

  5. ★

    In 1997 the sphere moved to the Adler Planetarium, which restored it to its 1913 look.
