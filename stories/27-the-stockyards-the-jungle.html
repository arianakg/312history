THE STOCKYARDS & 'THE JUNGLE'

For more than 100 years, one giant yard fed America — and changed its food laws.

◉ Back of the Yards (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Union Stock Yard opened on Christmas Day 1865.

  2. ★

    It covered 475 acres and handled more than one billion animals before it closed in 1971.

  3. ★

    Around 1921, about 40,000 people worked there, many of them immigrants.

  4. ★

    In 1906 Upton Sinclair's novel The Jungle shocked readers. That same year, Congress passed the Meat Inspection Act and the Pure Food and Drug Act. Sinclair wanted to help workers: "I aimed at the public's heart, and by accident I hit it in the stomach."

  5. ★

    Carl Sandburg called Chicago "Hog Butcher for the World" in his poem "Chicago" (1914).
