ESSANAY STUDIOS

Before Hollywood was the movie capital, Charlie Chaplin made a film on Chicago's North Side.

◉ Uptown, Argyle St (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1907, George K. Spoor and Gilbert M. Anderson founded a film company in Chicago and named it Essanay, for their initials, S and A.

  2. ★

    Around 1908, Essanay moved to a new studio on Argyle Street in Uptown, where it made hundreds of films.

  3. ★

    Anderson starred as "Broncho Billy," cinema's first cowboy hero.

  4. ★

    Essanay signed Charlie Chaplin for $1,250 a week. He shot only one film in Chicago, His New Job (1915), with a young Gloria Swanson as an extra.

  5. ★

    After Chaplin left and the founders fell out, Essanay collapsed in 1917. Its Argyle Street studio became a Chicago Landmark in 1996.
