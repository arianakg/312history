OZ WAS BORN IN CHICAGO

The yellow brick road started in Chicago: The Wonderful Wizard of Oz was written and published here.

◉ Humboldt Park (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    In 1899, Chicago writer L. Frank Baum and Chicago artist W. W. Denslow teamed up on Father Goose: His Book.

  2. ★

    Baum, who had moved to Chicago in 1891, wrote The Wonderful Wizard of Oz in 1899 at his home on Humboldt Boulevard.

  3. ★

    Chicago's George M. Hill Company published it in 1900 with Denslow's pictures. The first printing of 10,000 copies sold out.

  4. ★

    In a new idea for its time, parts of the text were printed right over Denslow's drawings.

  5. ★

    Baum turned the story into a stage musical that opened on June 16, 1902, at Chicago's Grand Opera House.
