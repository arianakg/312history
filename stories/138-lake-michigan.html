LAKE MICHIGAN

Lake Michigan is secretly HALF of a bigger lake?

◉ Lake Michigan, Chicago lakefront (map pin approximate)

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Lake Michigan is joined to Lake Huron at the Straits of Mackinac, and the two behave like one huge lake.

  2. ★

    It is the only Great Lake entirely inside the United States, bordered by Illinois, Indiana, Michigan and Wisconsin.

  3. ★

    It holds about 1,180 cubic miles of fresh water, second among the Great Lakes by volume.

  4. ★

    Its deepest point is about 925 feet down.

  5. ★

    Its shoreline, islands included, runs more than 1,600 miles.
