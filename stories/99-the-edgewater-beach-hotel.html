THE EDGEWATER BEACH HOTEL

From 1916 to 1967, the Edgewater Beach Hotel ran a lakefront resort on Sheridan Road.

◉ Edgewater Beach Hotel, Sheridan Rd (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The Edgewater Beach Hotel opened in June 1916 on Sheridan Road, right on the Lake Michigan shore.

  2. ★

    Architects Marshall & Fox gave it pink stucco walls and an X-shaped, Maltese-cross plan.

  3. ★

    The hotel complex had its own radio station, WEBH, named for the Edgewater Beach Hotel.

  4. ★

    In the early 1950s, Lake Shore Drive was extended north on landfill, cutting the hotel off from its beach.

  5. ★

    The hotel went bankrupt in 1967 and was torn down by 1971; its pink Edgewater Beach Apartments still stand.
