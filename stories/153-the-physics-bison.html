THE PHYSICS BISON

Why do BISON live at a physics lab?

◉ Fermilab bison pasture, Batavia (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Fermilab, the physics lab in Batavia about 35 miles from Chicago, has kept bison since 1969.

  2. ★

    First director Robert Wilson brought them in as a symbol of the frontier.

  3. ★

    Wilson grew up in Wyoming, on his family’s cattle ranch.

  4. ★

    The lab’s 6,800-acre site mixes giant particle accelerators with prairie, woods and wetlands.

  5. ★

    Genetic tests found the herd has little or no cattle DNA, rare among American bison today.
