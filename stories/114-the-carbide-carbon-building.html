THE CARBIDE & CARBON BUILDING

The Carbide and Carbon Building on Michigan Avenue is dressed in black granite, green terra cotta and gold.

◉ Carbide & Carbon Building, 230 N Michigan Ave (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    It was completed in 1929.

  2. ★

    Its lowest floors are faced with polished black granite.

  3. ★

    Above them, its walls are dark green terra cotta.

  4. ★

    Gold accents the top of its tower.

  5. ★

    It was built for the company that developed the first dry cell battery.
