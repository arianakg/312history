GLESSNER HOUSE

Behind its fortress-like stone walls, an 1887 Prairie Avenue house hides a sunny private courtyard.

◉ Prairie Ave (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Architect H. H. Richardson designed the house for farm-machinery executive John J. Glessner. It was built in 1886–1887 at Prairie Avenue and 18th Street.

  2. ★

    Its massive, rugged stone walls were built right at the edge of the lot, unlike the other houses on Prairie Avenue at the time.

  3. ★

    Its family rooms open onto a quiet inner courtyard, whose walls are full of doors and windows that let in light.

  4. ★

    The Glessners filled their home with Arts and Crafts design, including William Morris wallpapers and textiles.

  5. ★

    In 1966 preservationists bought the house for $35,000 to save it. It became a Chicago Landmark in 1970 and is now a house museum.
