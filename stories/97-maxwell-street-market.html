MAXWELL STREET MARKET

Maxwell Street Market became an official Chicago city market in 1912.

◉ Maxwell St & Halsted (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    The market grew up on the Near West Side in the late 1800s, started largely by Jewish immigrants from Eastern Europe.

  2. ★

    A city ordinance made it an official Chicago market in 1912.

  3. ★

    Peddlers sold goods from pushcarts and stands, from clothes and produce to appliances.

  4. ★

    Sunday was the big market day, drawing thousands of shoppers.

  5. ★

    In 1994 the city moved the market a few blocks east to Canal Street to make room for the University of Illinois at Chicago.
