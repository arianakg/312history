THE TOWER THAT SURVIVED

In 1871 the Great Fire burned all around it. The Water Tower is still standing.

◉ Michigan Ave (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    Built in 1869 by W. W. Boyington from Joliet limestone, it hid a 138-ft standpipe that steadied the water pressure.

  2. ★

    The fire of October 1871 swept through the North Side, but the stone tower held.

  3. ★

    The pumping station across the street lost its roof and pumps, and city water was nearly cut off for about 8 days.

  4. ★

    It survived demolition plans in 1906 and 1918, so Michigan Avenue bends around it. It became a city landmark in 1971.

Myth or fact? "It was the only building to survive the fire." Myth. A few others survived in the burned area, including the pumping station's walls, St. Michael's Church and a police officer's cottage.
