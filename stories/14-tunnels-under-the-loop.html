TUNNELS UNDER THE LOOP

A hidden railway once ran about 40 ft under downtown Chicago.

◉ Under the Loop (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    It began with an 1899 franchise for telephone cables. Tunnel digging started in 1900.

  2. ★

    Freight service began in 1906, and the network reached about 60 miles by 1909.

  3. ★

    Small electric trains on two-foot track carried rail freight, store packages, coal for boilers and ash for lakefront fill.

  4. ★

    Abandoned in 1959, a tunnel under the river was punctured on April 13, 1992, flooding about two dozen Loop buildings.

Myth or fact? "They were planned as freight tunnels from day one." Unclear. The 1899 franchise was for telephone cables. Whether trains were the plan all along is still debated.
