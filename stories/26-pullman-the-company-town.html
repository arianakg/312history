PULLMAN: THE COMPANY TOWN

A railroad-car king built a "perfect" town. It ended in a national strike.

◉ Pullman (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    From 1880 to 1884, George Pullman built a model town for the workers at his railroad-car factory.

  2. ★

    The company owned every house and shop, and the rents had to earn it a profit.

  3. ★

    In the 1893 depression, Pullman cut wages but not rents. On May 11, 1894, workers went on strike.

  4. ★

    Rail workers across the country boycotted Pullman cars: about 250,000 workers in 27 states. Federal troops ended the strike by mid-July.

  5. ★

    Congress made Labor Day a national holiday on June 28, 1894, in the middle of the strike crisis; more than 20 states already had one. In 2015, Pullman became a National Monument.
