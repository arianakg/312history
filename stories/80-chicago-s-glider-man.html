CHICAGO'S GLIDER MAN

Seven years before the Wright brothers flew, a Chicago engineer was testing gliders on the Indiana dunes.

◉ Indiana Dunes (outside Chicago) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    By 1894, Chicago civil engineer Octave Chanute, inspired by German glider pioneer Otto Lilienthal, was designing gliders that could carry a person.

  2. ★

    On June 22, 1896, Chanute and four helpers took the train from Chicago to the dunes at Miller, Indiana, now part of Gary, to test them.

  3. ★

    In August they camped farther east at Dune Park. A two-wing glider built with Augustus Herring made several hundred flights, the longest about 358 feet.

  4. ★

    On May 13, 1900, Wilbur Wright wrote to Chanute for the first time. About 435 letters and telegrams passed between them over the next decade.

  5. ★

    Chanute visited the Wrights' camp at Kitty Hawk and stayed a friend and adviser to the brothers. He died in 1910.
