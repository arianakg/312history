THE 41ST STREET BRIDGE

Why does this South Side bridge snake like an S?

◉ 41st Street Bridge, Bronzeville lakefront (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    It opened in December 2018, linking Bronzeville to the lakefront and the new 41st Street Beach.

  2. ★

    The S-shaped span is 1,470 feet long and crosses the commuter rail tracks and Lake Shore Drive.

  3. ★

    It is fully accessible, with ramps for bikes, wheelchairs and even emergency vehicles.

  4. ★

    Its blue steel arches are inclined, leaning at an angle instead of standing upright.

  5. ★

    Its design grew out of Bridging the Drive, a city design competition for lakefront bridges.
