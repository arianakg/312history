POETRY MAGAZINE

A Chicago writer asked her city to pay for poems, and started a magazine that is still publishing.

◉ Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Chicago-born writer Harriet Monroe composed the dedication ode for the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition.

  2. ★

    In 1911 and 1912 she asked Chicago patrons to pledge $50 a year for five years for a new magazine. More than 100 said yes.

  3. ★

    Poetry: A Magazine of Verse debuted in 1912. Its motto came from Walt Whitman: "To have great poets there must be great audiences too." It paid every poet it printed.

  4. ★

    Poetry printed early important poems by Carl Sandburg, T. S. Eliot, Ezra Pound, Marianne Moore and Wallace Stevens. Sandburg's poem "Chicago" appeared there in 1914.

  5. ★

    Monroe edited Poetry for 24 years, until she died in 1936 while traveling in Peru. The magazine is still published in Chicago.
