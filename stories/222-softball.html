1887

SOFTBALL

Did you know softball was invented in Chicago?

◉ 3016–3018 S. Lake Park Ave., Chicago (former Farragut Boat Club) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    Did you know softball was invented in Chicago?

  2. ★

    On Thanksgiving Day in 1887, friends were at the Farragut Boat Club

  3. ★

    Someone tossed a boxing glove. Another person hit it with a broom

  4. ★

    George Hancock wrote a set of rules and called the new game indoor baseball

  5. ★

    The game later moved outside and became softball. Chicago still loves its 16-inch game
