THE TUCKER 48

In 1948, a Chicago factory built a car with a third headlight that turned with the steering wheel.

◉ Tucker Corporation plant, 7401 S. Cicero Ave, Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    It was built in Chicago in 1947–48.

  2. ★

    Its center “Cyclops” headlight turned with the steering.

  3. ★

    Its rear engine was adapted from a helicopter engine.

  4. ★

    It had a padded dashboard for safety.

  5. ★

    Only 51 were ever made.
