AL CAPONE: MYTH VS FACT

America's most famous gangster went to prison for taxes — not murder.

◉ Chicago (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 4 FACTS

  1. ★

    Prohibition (1920–1933) banned selling alcohol. Illegal liquor made gangs rich, and the city violent.

  2. ★

    Capone, born in Brooklyn in 1899, came to run a violent Chicago gang.

  3. ★

    Federal agents followed the money. In October 1931 he was convicted of tax evasion and sentenced to 11 years.

  4. ★

    He was sent to Alcatraz in 1934, released in 1939, and died in 1947.

Myth or fact? "Capone went to prison for the St. Valentine's Day Massacre." Myth. No one was ever convicted for the 1929 killings. Capone went to prison for not paying his taxes.
