ELI'S CHEESECAKE

This Chicago cheesecake started at a steak restaurant

◉ 6701 W. Forest Preserve Dr. (Eli's Cheesecake) (map pin approximate)

Follow on Instagram ★
Watch the reel

★ 5 FACTS

  1. ★

    This Chicago cheesecake started at a steak restaurant

  2. ★

    Eli Schulman opened a steak restaurant in 1966

  3. ★

    It became famous at the first Taste of Chicago in 1980

  4. ★

    They made a 2,000-pound cake for a president's inauguration

  5. ★

    The bakery is still open at 6701 West Forest Preserve Drive
