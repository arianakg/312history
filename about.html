WHO IS BEHIND 312 HISTORY

Hi, I'm Ariana Cholponkadyrova, the creator of 312 History. I'm from Kyrgyzstan, and Chicago is now my home.

Back in Kyrgyzstan I worked on vocational and technical education projects. In agriculture I helped advance the green economy: we built greenhouses and ran green-economy courses. I was the coordinator and organizer of Karagat Fest, the annual berry and fruit festival of the Issyk-Kul region, and worked as a PR specialist on many projects. My background is in event management and a major PR agency.

In Chicago I'm a content creator and a mom of two: my daughter is 5 and my son is 9. I also run a small business printing designs on T-shirts and tote bags, a craft I love. I enjoy photography and making beautiful videos, and right now I'm studying AI neuro-content and AI agents.

MISSION
