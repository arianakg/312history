About the project

CHICAGO HISTORYWITH FRESHEYES★

MISSION

Uncovering Chicago's history with the fresh eyes of someone who chose this city as home.

When I moved to Chicago, I didn't know its streets, its neighborhoods, or its past. But every building I passed seemed to hold a secret. Who lived here a hundred years ago? Who built this city?

I'm Ariana Cholponkadyrova. Back home in Kyrgyzstan, I worked as a PR manager at a public foundation that ran large projects in agriculture, the green economy and vocational and technical education, and I coordinated Karagat Fest, the annual berry and fruit festival of the Issyk-Kul region. I moved to Chicago four years ago and started over as a mom of two and a content creator. I love making beautiful photos and videos, I create AI content and study AI, and I'm still improving my English. Most of all, I love building brands from scratch. Exploring my new city became my way of making it home.

That's how 312history was born.

WHAT IS 312HISTORY?

312history tells the story of Chicago through short videos and an interactive map. Every story is a point on the map, so you can discover what happened right on your block. Explore it at 312history.com and follow along on Instagram.

WHY FRESH EYES?

Chicago was built by people who came from far away: Polish, German, Irish, Mexican, Ukrainian and many more. As someone who chose this city as home, I see their stories in a personal way. Their story is my story too.

WHAT'S NEXT?

More neighborhoods, more forgotten heroes, and every story in both English and Russian. My dream is that one day anyone walking down a Chicago street can open the map and hear the voice of the past.

If you love Chicago, or have ever built a new home far from where you started, join me. Let's discover this city together.

VALUES

  • Truth. Only checked facts, with a source in every post.
  • People. History told through human lives, not dry dates.
  • Openness. Chicago for everyone who came here, in Russian and English.
  • Curiosity. We look for secrets and forgotten stories close to home.
  • Respect. We speak with care about tragedies and different cultures.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

  1. A fresh, personal view from someone who chose Chicago as home, coming from Kyrgyzstan.
  2. Two languages, Russian and English, for an audience almost no one serves.
  3. An interactive map on the site: every story is a point on the map.
  4. Experience in PR and events: we know how to create events, partnerships and press.

Chicago history, one story at a time. Every fact checked against sources.
