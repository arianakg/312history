About the project

CHICAGO HISTORYTHROUGH ANIMMIGRANT'S EYES★

MISSION

To tell the history of Chicago through an immigrant's eyes, so that everyone who comes here can feel that this city is their own.

HI, I'M ARIANA

I'm Ariana Cholponkadyrova, the creator of 312 History. I came to Chicago from Kyrgyzstan, and exploring the history of my new city became my way of making it home.

Back home I worked on vocational and technical education projects. In agriculture I helped advance the green economy: we built greenhouses and ran green-economy courses. I was the coordinator and organizer of Karagat Fest, the annual berry and fruit festival of the Issyk-Kul region, and a PR specialist on many projects. Event management and a major PR agency taught me how to bring people together around a good story.

In Chicago I'm a content creator and a mom of two: my daughter is 5 and my son is 9. I run a small business printing designs on T-shirts and tote bags, a craft I truly love. I love photography and making beautiful videos, and right now I'm studying AI neuro-content and AI agents.

VISION

312 History becomes the leading bilingual guide to Chicago's history: videos, the map at 312history.com, tours and a book that connect the city's past with the people who live here today.

Today that means short videos on Instagram and an interactive map where every story is a point you can visit.

VALUES

  • Truth. Only checked facts, with a source in every post.
  • People. History told through human lives, not dry dates.
  • Openness. Chicago for everyone who came here, in Russian and English.
  • Curiosity. We look for secrets and forgotten stories close to home.
  • Respect. We speak with care about tragedies and different cultures.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

  1. The view of an immigrant from Kyrgyzstan: fresh and personal.
  2. Two languages, Russian and English, for an audience almost no one serves.
  3. An interactive map on the site: every story is a point on the map.
  4. Experience in PR and events: we know how to create events, partnerships and press.
