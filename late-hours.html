CHICAGO'S4 A.M. BARS★

The city lists 111 late-hour licenses. We found 1 has closed, so here are the 110 we could confirm, listed by community area, from the far North Side to Beverly. 98 sit north of Madison Street, only 12 sit south.

This list comes from current city late-hour licenses. Closed places were removed. Late-hour licenses let a place serve drinks until 4 a.m. Monday through Saturday, and until 5 a.m. on Sunday. Hours can change — check before you go. These are not every bar in the city. Most bars must stop serving at 2 a.m. (3 a.m. on Sunday).
